# ⚔️ Pokémon

## 🧠 Meaning

Catch, build, battle — the team and the competition are the point, not the checklist. Competing against strong teams is the endless pursuit; the grind (dex tail, brute-force shiny) is the trap.

> 🚪 **Retired from Nintendo from the Wii U era onward** — titles marked 🔒 **launched after** the Wii era and were not played. Story done through Gen IV, dex ~80%.

## 🧠 What the games taught

- **Type chart** — super effective (2×), not very effective (0.5×), immunities (0×). The whole fight loop is **swap to the weakness** while managing resources.
- **HM coverage** — a team built to carry the required moves navigates the entire game; no mid-run swaps needed.
- **EVs** — effort values summarize what a Pokémon actually experienced in battle; training focus shapes stats.
- **Resource discipline** — items are an emergency lane, not a crutch; the no-item Elite League is the real test.

## 🥚 Breeding

**Ditto** breeds with almost any egg group — eggs inherit an IV spread from the parents, so Ditto plus a good partner propagates stats. Perfect IVs are the competitive breeding target.

## ✨ Shiny hunting

Different palette, very rare — ~1/8192 in Gen III–IV wild encounters; **Masuda method** improves the odds via breeding. The fun is real, but pure random encounter + run repeats 4k+ times and is easy
to miss the target — use a method next time.

## 🎭 Contests & berries

Pokémon contests and berry customization never entertained — didn't understand the appeal of dressing up and performing with creatures built to fight. Skipped, and that was the right call.
