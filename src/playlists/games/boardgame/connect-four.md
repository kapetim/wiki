# 🔴 Connect 4

**The drop game.** Line up four first — the lesson is threat reading and zugzwang.

## 🧠 Meaning

The board is decided by threats, not moves; whoever forces a pattern wins.

## 🗝️ Beat it with ease

- **Take the center column.** It's in every diagonal and horizontal — the only spot that can threaten from the first move.
- **Create double threats.** A move that sets up two winning lines at once is unstoppable — your opponent can block only one.
- **Block, then extend.** After blocking, always check if your blocking piece itself creates a new threat.
- **Force the opponent to fill columns.** If you can make the third move in a column, the fourth is yours or a threat.
- **Zugzwang is the win.** When every column is bad for the opponent, their forced move opens your win — play to exhaust their safe columns.
- **Mirror the center early.** Don't let the opponent own the middle; respond in the center column immediately.
