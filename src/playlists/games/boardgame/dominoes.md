# 🁢 Dominoes

**The blocking game.** Empty your hand and read the table — the lesson is counting and control.

## 🧠 Meaning

Whoever controls the open ends controls the game; counting tiles turns luck into math.

## 🗝️ Beat it with ease

- **Count the pips.** Track every tile played and the numbers remaining in hand; the open ends are the whole game.
- **Feed the suit you hold.** Play the number you still carry tiles for, so you never get stuck without a match.
- **Block the heavy suit.** If the opponent is holding doubles of a number, keep that suit closed on the table.
- **Empty your high tiles first.** The double-six is dead weight at the end — shed it while the table is open.
- **Force the draw.** If your play leaves the opponent unmatched, they pay — pick plays that close their options.
- **Endgame: keep one of each.** Holding both open numbers late means you always have a move; the opponent stalls first.
