# 🏢 Monopoly

**The money game.** Buy, build, and bleed opponents dry — the lesson is cash management under pressure.

## 🧠 Meaning

Ownership is a machine; houses are the throttle. Never let the machine stall for lack of rent.

## 🗝️ Beat it with ease

- **Buy orange and red first.** Most landed-on squares of the board; the orange set is the highest-value cheap purchase.
- **Houses over hotels.** Three houses on a set earn more than one hotel for the same cash; build wide before building tall.
- **Never break your cash reserve.** Keep enough to pay rent; bankrupting yourself on a single roll is the real loss.
- **Trade to complete sets.** Two useless properties are worth one complete set to someone else — swap for the third piece.
- **Jail is a parking lot.** Mid-game, staying in jail avoids the expensive tail of the board — don't rush to pay bail.
- **Count the doubles.** You roll out of jail on a double; know when the exit is forced and which squares that exposes.
