# 💥 The Meeseeks

**What  this  is:**  collective  —  the  Meeseeks  species.  The  servant  box's  blue  helpers  —  "I'm  Mr.  Meeseeks,  look  at  me!"  —  beings  summoned  to  solve  one  task  and  exist  only
until  it's  done,  which  is  exactly  why  they  go  murderously  mad  when  the  task  drags.

**The read:** the Meeseeks are servant-horror comedy — beings whose entire existence is a to-do list, and whose desperation grows with the task's difficulty. The "help Jerry gain
confidence" episode turns their logic into an army.
