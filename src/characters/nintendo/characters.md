## 🔀 Similarities — the pipes ↔ shortcut axis

One axis runs through the Mario cast — **effort/adventure ↔ shortcut/greed**. Mario and Luigi enter the pipes in pursuit of Peach and the stars; Wario swaps quick games with no meaning and
jumps to the solution; Waluigi is the far pole — chaos with no prize.
