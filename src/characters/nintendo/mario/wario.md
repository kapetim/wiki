## 🪞 Appearance

Short, stout, and heavy — a brawler's frame, not a hero's. The mustache and yellow/purple read as *anti-Mario*: same silhouette, opposite intent.

## 🗣️ Signature

- "Wah!" — the brag that doubles as a war cry
- Garlic, gold, and the shoulder-barge
- Tells: goes for the shortcut first, then makes it work by force

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the fastest clean win is a shortcut and the prize is yours to grab.
- **Borrow:** the refusal to grind a long adventure when a quick game pays the same.
- **Avoid:** the greed trap — the shortcut that costs more than the long road.
