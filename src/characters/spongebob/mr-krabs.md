## 🗣️ Signature

- The Scottish-tinged bark — every sentence a negotiation
- Money metaphors for everything — love, time, and family priced in dollars
- Tells: the eyes go to cash-register symbols when he hears a coin

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you must read the person who treats everything as a transaction — except the one or two things they truly love.
- **Borrow:** the margin read — know what the asset is worth before you negotiate.
- **Avoid:** the greed trap — a business that pinches its own workers' loyalty eventually runs out of workers.
