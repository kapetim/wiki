## 🗣️ Signature

- The patient "meow" — a full language in one syllable
- Watches the chaos with the stillness of someone who's seen it all before
- Tells: the meow rises a pitch when SpongeBob's about to do something very stupid

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the loud ones are running the room and someone needs to watch, quietly, and stay ready.
- **Borrow:** the witness read — say little, see everything, act only when it truly counts.
- **Avoid:** the silence trap — competence nobody knows about is competence that never gets called on.
