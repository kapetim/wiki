## 🗣️ Signature

- The tiny, furious voice — evil with a napoleon complex you can hold in your hand
- Monologues every plan to the enemy, unprompted
- Tells: the twitch when someone calls him "little"

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you must understand persistence — the underdog who never wins and never quits.
- **Borrow:** the persistence read — refusal to stop is its own kind of power, even against stacked odds.
- **Avoid:** the ego trap — announcing the plan to the target is how the microscopic villain stays microscopic.
