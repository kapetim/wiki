## 🗣️ Signature

- The muffled, unhearable voice under the parka — "mmph mmph!"
- Dies with a shrug and a one-liner, back next week like nothing happened
- Tells: as Mysterion, the same loyalty, suddenly audible

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the joke is counting you out — and you're the one who'll be back anyway.
- **Borrow:** the comeback read — treat the loss as a gag, not an ending.
- **Avoid:** the erasure trap — if no one remembers you fell, make sure they remember you rose.
