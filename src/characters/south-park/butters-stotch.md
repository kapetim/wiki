## 🗣️ Signature

- The sunny voice that never learns to be bitter — "oh hamburgers!"
- Cheerful compliance hiding real hurt
- Tells: as Professor Chaos, the same gentle sincerity aimed at villainy

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the world keeps punishing you and staying kind is still the right move.
- **Borrow:** the resilience read — let the hurt show in your catchphrase, not your character.
- **Avoid:** the naivety trap — trust is beautiful until it's the plan's entry point.
