## 🗣️ Signature

- The high, scheming voice — every sentence a plan, every compliment a trap
- Loves himself loudly, everyone else transactionally
- Tells: the smile when he thinks he's won — which is almost always

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you must see the pure drive of the person who'll do anything to get what they want.
- **Borrow:** the read — never underestimate the one with no filter and no brakes.
- **Avoid:** the id engine — Cartman wins is the show's definition of failure; winning like him means the cynicism became normal.
