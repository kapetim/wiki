# 🖤 The Goth Kids

**What  this  is:**  collective  —  South  Park's  goth  kids.  The  counter-culture  clique  that  hates  everything  —  including,  especially,  the  vampire  kids  who  think  they're  dark.
Grouped  as  one  file:  their  satire  is  the  group.

<!-- begin table -->
| Member | Role | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Michael | the lead goth | The deadpan ringleader — misery as identity |
| Henrietta Biggle | the goth girl | The angriest poet in the group — hates her parents, loves the misery |
| Pete | the quiet goth | Broods on schedule — the group's texture, not its voice |
| Firkle | the junior goth | The little one trying to keep up — later seduced by the vampires |
<!-- end table -->

**The read:** the goths are counter-culture satire that secretly cares — they hate everything and are devastated when something real threatens the scene. The vampire-kids arc
("The Ungroundable") splits them: goths reject the wannabes who made darkness a costume.
