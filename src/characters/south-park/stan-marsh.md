## 🗣️ Signature

- The flat, world-weary delivery — the kid who's already seen it all by third grade
- "That's so stupid" as the show's moral compass in two words
- Tells: the sigh before the take

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** someone needs to say the obvious thing the room is too scared to say.
- **Borrow:** the everyman read — you don't need genius, just the refusal to accept stupidity.
