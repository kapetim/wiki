## 🗣️ Signature

- The warm, gravelly voice — every piece of advice delivered like a hook
- Sings the answers instead of lecturing them
- Tells: he actually listens, which makes him the rarest adult in town

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the kids (or your team) need one adult who's on their side without an agenda.
- **Borrow:** the mentor read — be straight, be warm, and let them decide.
- **Avoid:** the disposability trap — even the irreplaceable need the institution to back them, or they're gone with the voice actor.
