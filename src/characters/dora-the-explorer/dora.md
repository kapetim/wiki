## 🗣️ Signature

- The bright, questioning voice — "¿Dónde está?" as a way of life
- Asks the audience like they're the bravest person in the room
- Tells: the pause that waits for the answer she already knows

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the path is unknown and the way forward is asking instead of assuming.
- **Borrow:** the questioner read — curiosity that includes the room turns followers into co-pilots.
- **Avoid:** the trust trap — openness is a strength; just keep one eye out for the fox.
