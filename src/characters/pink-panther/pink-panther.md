## 🗣️ Signature

- Silence as a language — the panther never speaks, never needs to
- The cool walk — every movement unhurried, every escape inevitable
- Tells: the glance, the shrug, the slip-through that ends the chase

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the pressure is rising and the cool head wins the room.
- **Borrow:** the composure read — never let the chase see you rush; dignity is a defense.
- **Avoid:** the ease trap — cool that never engages is cool that never builds anything either.
