## 🗣️ Signature

- The mangled accent delivering total certainty — every guess a masterpiece to him
- Stumbles through crime scenes leaving solved cases behind
- Tells: the triumphant pose right after he's catastrophically wrong

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** confidence is doing the work and outcomes are somehow landing anyway.
- **Borrow:** the oblivious-momentum read — not knowing you can fail is occasionally a working strategy.
- **Avoid:** the delusion trap — luck that looks like genius runs out the moment someone audits the method.
