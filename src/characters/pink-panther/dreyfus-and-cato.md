# 🎯 Dreyfus & Cato

**What this is:** collective — Clouseau's foil and his manservant. The film lane's two constants around the bungler: the chief who wants him gone, and the servant whose loyalty means attacking him.

<!-- begin table -->
| Member | Role | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chief Inspector Dreyfus | the boss | The chief driven mad by Clouseau's success — every scheme to end him backfires |
| Cato | the manservant | The kung-fu servant — attacks Clouseau on sight, per orders, with love |
<!-- end table -->

**The  read:**  the  two  are  Clouseau's  permanent  consequences  —  Dreyfus  the  authority  that  can't  stop  him,  Cato  the  home  that  can't  be  safe.  **Note:**  Clouseau  was  recast
across  eras  (Sellers  ·  Arkin  ·  Moore  ·  Steve  Martin);  the  character  file  covers  the  role,  not  one  actor.
