## 🗣️ Signature

- The laugh — "heh-heh-heh-heh-heh!" — irritation made audible
- Peck, dodge, vanish — the woodpecker's toolkit
- Tells: he provokes first and enjoys the chase more than the win

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** authority needs its feathers ruffled and the room needs the tension broken.
- **Borrow:** the cue read — announce yourself; sometimes the reputation does half the work.
- **Avoid:** the cycle trap — provoking for its own sake never builds anything; make the trouble mean something or it's just noise.
