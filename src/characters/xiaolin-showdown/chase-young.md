## 🗣️ Signature

- Calm, mocking, precise — the villain who never raises his voice
- Fights beside the monks before he betrays them, so the offer always feels earned
- Tells: the tea — an offer of power always comes with a cup

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you must read the mentor who's selling something — the easy path with a handsome face.
- **Borrow:** the patience read — the corruptor wins by waiting for the ego to open the door.
- **Avoid:** the easy-power trap — the person who flatters your potential is usually pricing your loyalty.
