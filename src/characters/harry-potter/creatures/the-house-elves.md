# 🧤 The House-Elves

**What  this  is:**  collective  —  the  house-elves  of  the  wizarding  world.  The  bound  servants  whose  magic  keeps  the  castles  running  —  a  species  defined  by  the  masters  they
serve.  [Dobby.md](dobby.md)  has  his  own  file  (the  one  who  chose  freedom).

<!-- begin table -->
| Member | Service | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kreacher | The Blacks | The bitter elf — hateful, loyal to a dead mistress, redeemed by the master he finally accepted |
| Winky | The Crouches | The heartbroken elf — freedom as a punishment she never wanted |
<!-- end table -->

**The read:** the house-elves are the wizarding world's quietest injustice — slavery wrapped in gratitude — and the series' treatment of them is one of its messiest threads
(Hermione's S.P.E.W. as the comic-uneasy conscience).
