# 🏡 The Dursleys

**What  this  is:**  collective  —  Harry's  Muggle  family.  The  household  at  Number  Four,  Privet  Drive  —  the  ordinary  family  that  raised  the  Boy  Who  Lived  as  an  inconvenience,
and  the  ordinary  cruelty  he  escaped  to  Hogwarts.

<!-- begin table -->
| Member | Role | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vernon Dursley | the father | The bulldog Muggle — order, normalcy, and the nephew who threatens both |
| Petunia Dursley | the mother | The resentful sister — Lily's magic as the wound she never healed |
| Dudley Dursley | the son | The spoiled cousin — the bully who learned, in the end, to say thank you |
<!-- end table -->

**The read:** the Dursleys are the anti-Hogwarts — the "normal" world at its most ordinary and cruel, the baseline that makes the magic home so clearly the real family.
