## 🗣️ Signature

- The dreamy, certain voice — statements delivered like she's reading the sky
- Names the truth sideways — the thing everyone avoids, said gently
- Tells: the Radish Earrings — armor disguised as whimsy

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the room is too sensible to see what's real, and someone needs to say it weirdly.
- **Borrow:** the believer read — hold your own reality; it's often the sanest thing in the room.
- **Avoid:** the detachment trap — staying in your own world forever is how the grief you postponed comes back unprocessed.
