## 🗣️ Signature

- Deadpan, jealous, fiercely loyal — jokes hiding how much he cares
- The chessboard mind that sees the whole game
- Tells: second-guesses himself, then does the brave thing anyway

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you're the one who thinks he's second-best but shows up anyway.
- **Borrow:** loyalty that outlives the envy — be the friend who faces the board for them.
- **Avoid:** the spare trap — measuring yourself against siblings and legends forgets the wand you already hold.
