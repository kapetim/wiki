## 🗣️ Signature

- The warm, commanding voice — every question a demand for information about your health
- Knits, cooks, and deploys Howlers with equal ferocity
- Tells: the moment the smile drops — usually aimed at a Death Eater

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the group needs the one whose love converts instantly into protection.
- **Borrow:** the fierce-love read — nurture and defense are the same instinct, tuned differently.
- **Avoid:** the smother trap — the care that never lets the grown child leave is a love that forgets to trust.
