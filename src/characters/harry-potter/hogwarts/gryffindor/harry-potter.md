## 🗣️ Signature

- Blunt, brave, allergic to subtlety — speaks the truth in the face of a basilisk
- The scar that aches when evil is near
- Tells: rushes in where angels fear — and it usually works

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need courage that acts before the fear finishes the sentence.
- **Borrow:** love as the whole answer — the people you'd die for are the point.
- **Avoid:** the savior trap — carrying every fight alone forgets the friends standing beside you.
