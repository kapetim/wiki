## 🗣️ Signature

- Precise, relentless, slightly terrified of being second-best
- Raises her hand and her standards
- Tells: brandishes a book like a weapon; lectures monsters mid-fight

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need preparation and principle — be the one who did the reading.
- **Borrow:** preparation beats luck; and when the rules are wrong, break them for the right reason.
- **Avoid:** the pride trap — the know-it-all who can't admit she's scared misses the help right beside her.
