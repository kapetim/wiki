## 🗣️ Signature

- The drawling sneer — every insult a small performance of superiority
- Cowardice worn as self-preservation
- Tells: the mask drops the second his father's approval is at stake

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you must read the person raised to be the villain who never got a choice.
- **Borrow:** the mask read — the sneer is armor; find the fear under it before you judge.
- **Avoid:** the legacy trap — living up to a name that asks you to become a killer is how the boy becomes the monster.
