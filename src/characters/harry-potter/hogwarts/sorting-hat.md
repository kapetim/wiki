house  where  they'll  grow  best.  A  character,  not  a  prop.

## 🗣️ Signature

- Sings a fresh song each start-of-term — the school's founding truth put to verse
- Dusty, blunt, wise — judgment that reads like being *seen*
- Tells: names not just the house, but the self the newcomer hasn't admitted yet

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** sizing someone up for where they'd *thrive* — the placement read, not the label read.
- **Borrow:** see potential, then let the person's own choice finish the sorting.
- **Avoid:** the first-impression verdict — the hat nearly made Harry a Slytherin on a reading that missed who he'd choose to be.
