# 👻 The Hogwarts Ghosts

**What  this  is:**  collective  —  the  castle's  dead.  The  ghosts  who  haunt  Hogwarts'  corridors  —  each  house's  resident  spirit,  the  poltergeist  who  isn't  a  ghost  at  all,  and
the  ones  who  carry  the  school's  memory.

<!-- begin table -->
| Member | House | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nearly Headless Nick | Gryffindor | The beheaded knight — the failed execution that defines him |
| The Grey Lady | Ravenclaw | The keeper of the diadem — the ghost who hid the Horcrux |
| The Bloody Baron | Slytherin | The violent legend — the house ghost who frightens even the living |
| The Fat Friar | Hufflepuff | The cheerful spirit — Hufflepuff's gentle dead |
| Moaning Myrtle | (bathroom) | The murdered girl — the witness to the Chamber's secrets |
| Peeves | (not a ghost) | The poltergeist — chaos the living can't exorcise |
<!-- end table -->

**The read:** the ghosts are the castle's memory layer — the dead who keep the school's history (and its secrets) walking the corridors.
