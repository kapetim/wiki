## 🗣️ Signature

- The old voice that goes young — energy saved for the one thing worth spending it on
- Tells tall stories like they're investments
- Tells: he's out of bed before Charlie finishes the sentence

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you've been saving your fire and someone finally gives you a reason to spend it.
- **Borrow:** the second-wind read — enthusiasm is a resource; deploy it on the right person's dream.
- **Avoid:** the instigator trap — the elder who leads the kid into the risk should also be the one who gets him out.
