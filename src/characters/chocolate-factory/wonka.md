## 🎞️ Version read

<!-- begin table -->
| Version | Portrayal | Where the lesson lands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1971 · Wilder | The sardonic trickster — cynical, charming, distrustful of parents; no backstory | The test is pure: will you trade family for the candy? |
| 2005 · Depp | The wounded child-man — the dentist-father flashbacks, the recluse who never grew past the exile | The test is therapy: Charlie's refusal forces Wonka to face his own father |
<!-- end table -->

**Slugworth note:** the 1971-only spy subplot (Slugworth tempting Charlie with the gobstopper) is a scripted test of integrity that the 2005 version folds into the tour's hidden cameras.

## 🗣️ Signature

- Flips between host, salesman, and wounded child — the register changes with the version
- Speaks in riddles and product names
- Tells: the sudden stillness when family comes up

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you built the system and you're testing who's worthy to inherit it.
- **Borrow:** the room-as-mirror read — design the test so the trait reveals itself.
- **Avoid:** the isolation trap — sealing yourself off to avoid the wound just hands the healing to a stranger's refusal.
