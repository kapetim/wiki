## 🎞️ Evaluating both versions — the manner

Two films, one factory — each adapts the same lottery through a different Wonka. The manner: compare each axis, note where each version *lands the lesson*, keep open what you haven't
decided. Per-character detail lives in each dossier's `🎞️ Version read` table.

<!-- begin table -->
| Axis | 1971 · Wilder | 2005 · Burton | Read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wonka's nature | The sardonic trickster — cynical, charming, no backstory | The wounded child-man — dentist-father flashbacks, a recluse | 1971 makes the test pure; 2005 makes it therapy |
| Grandpa Joe | The lively musical partner — sings, leads the fizzy-lifting risk | The quieter guide — support, not instigator | 1971 shares the mischief; 2005 keeps Charlie central |
| The four vices | Blunt archetypes — pure appetite, blunt greed | Bigger staging + the parents made complicit | 1971 punishes the kid; 2005 critiques the parents too |
| Mike Teavee's vice | TV — the broadcast shrinks the boy | Games/internet — the datamine updates the same lack of embodiment | Same lesson, era-reskinned |
| Oompa-Loompas | Orange/green varied singers — full moralizing numbers | CGI uniform dancer-crew — production over song | The chorus turns into spectacle |
| Songs | The musical — Pure Imagination, the Oompa numbers | Scored and stylized — fewer sing-along beats | 1971 carries the whimsy on melody |
| Tone | Gentle-dark whimsy | Surreal-gothic carnival | 2005 leans into the wound, 1971 into the wink |
| Ending | Charlie refuses; family accepted, no backstory | Charlie refuses; Wonka reconciles with his father | Both: family beats the empire; 2005 closes Wonka's loop |
<!-- end table -->
