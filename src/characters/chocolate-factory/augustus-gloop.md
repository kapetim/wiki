## 🗣️ Signature

- Talks with his mouth full — every sentence an interruption of a meal
- Treats the factory like an all-you-can-eat
- Tells: he never looks up from the food long enough to see the danger

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you must read the person whose whole model is "more is better."
- **Borrow:** the intake read — volume strategies win some lotteries; know when they stop scaling.
- **Avoid:** the no-brake trap — desire without a satiety model ends in a pipe, not a throne.
