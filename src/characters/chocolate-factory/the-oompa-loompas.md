# 🟠 The Oompa-Loompas

**What  this  is:**  collective  —  the  factory's  workforce.  Wonka's  mysterious  little  crew  —  the  chorus  that  sings  the  lessons  as  each  vice  is  eliminated,  and  one  of  the
clearest  version-divergences  between  the  films.

<!-- begin table -->
| Member | 1971 · Wilder | 2005 · Burton |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Oompa-Loompas | Orange skin, green hair, varied heights — the singing chorus with full moralizing numbers | CGI, uniform height, striped outfits — the dancer-crew, more production than song |
<!-- end table -->

**The read:** the Oompa-Loompas are the factory's moral theater — the ones who turn each elimination into a public lesson with a rhythm.
