## 🗣️ Signature

- The imperious squeal — "I want it NOW" as the whole vocabulary
- Treats the father like a vending machine
- Tells: the tantrum scale rises exactly with the size of the "no"

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you must understand the person raised to believe every want is a demand.
- **Borrow:** the leverage read — know what money can and can't clear before you bet the market on it.
- **Avoid:** the entitlement trap — demanding what isn't for sale is how the entitled get sorted out of the room.
