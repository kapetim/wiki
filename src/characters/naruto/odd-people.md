## 🔮 Possibilities and lineage limits

The anime plays with possibilities — each generation shows them during war, then gets limited by the kekkei
genkai.

- **An Uchiha having a baby with a Byakugan user is not feasible** — it would make a Rinnegan holder in the
  village, someone impossible to control who puts the Hokage in check.
- **Male Uchiha are forbidden in the village** — the village fears what could happen.
- If your goal is to command everyone, a Rinnegan holder is trouble; the village prefers to believe Pain
  died and commands everyone else.
- The Hokage is the **7-gold** card — he prefers to live as the best and forget the other three; there may be
  more than three, judging by how populated the orphanage is.
- **Kabuto and the orphanage** — the village probably trusts Kabuto after he stopped the war alone. The
  orphanage also reminds Sarada why she must keep training when village life bores her.

## ⚔️ The Shisui war origin

The anime probably started with a war around Shisui — the claim that not even the Hokage could convince him.
That became a bait to make the Hokage believe he was dead, while Shisui's eye kept working, masked as one of
Itachi's crows. The Hokage is too old to spot it and does not want to review crows to find him; he kept
believing his stolen eye worked, yet no one obeyed him.

The narrative is **obey my command or I steal your eye** — and Shisui somewhat won against him.

- **The teleportation question** — what stopped him from simply teleporting after casting a genjutsu? The
  teleportation guy would stand still, waiting to be attacked by everyone else.
- **The stupid army** — a delusional leader creating an army of people fooled their whole life into silly
  strategies that harm no one. Some people can only obey through pain.
