## 🗣️ Signature

- Blasphemous preacher's energy — praises Jashin mid-battle
- No fear register — swings his scythe into his own blood to share the wound
- Tells: the ritual circle; treats his body like a resource, not a self

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you want the audacity of someone with nothing to lose.
- **Borrow:** total commitment to a cause — risk that looks insane from outside.
- **Avoid:** the immortality trap — a fighter who can't die still loses to someone who can think.
