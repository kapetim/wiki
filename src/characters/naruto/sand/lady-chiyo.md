## 🗣️ Signature

- Old-soldier calm — has seen every war and stopped being impressed
- Puppets and poison as the Sand's old language
- Tells: hides tenderness behind dry wit; carries her grief in her craft

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you want the weight of someone who's seen it all and still chooses to fix it.
- **Borrow:** owning your legacy — even the part you created and regret.
- **Avoid:** the atonement trap — a life spent undoing your own poison may only end when you give the rest of it away.
