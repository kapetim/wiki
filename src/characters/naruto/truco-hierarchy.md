### 🔀 The wildcard — Sasuke

Sasuke never became chūnin or jōnin. He skipped the entire rank ladder, tied Naruto at the Valley of the End, and left the
village. The Ace value appears in two places in Truco — common Ace below 1, and Espadilha among the trumps. This mirrors
Sasuke: unranked in the village system but immensely valuable to it. He stimulates Naruto (7 ♦) to keep improving. He
attracts Sakura precisely because he is the only one who ignored her.

### 🪞 Training mirrors

Itachi (7 ♥) always beat Sasuke (A ♠). Shisui (4 ♣) always beat Itachi. Each higher card tracks progress through consistent
wins over the one below. The same dynamic applies to Sasuke and Naruto: the wildcard stimulates the leader to improve, and
their rivalry keeps both moving forward.

### 🔁 Circle of influence

Elders → Shisui (4 ♣) → Itachi (7 ♥) → Sasuke (A ♠) → Naruto (7 ♦) → village ranks

Shisui may have thrown a genjutsu on Itachi, who believed it and acted on Sasuke, who believed it and disappeared. Naruto
also believed the story. The hidden card at the top fabricated his death and the chain of belief cascaded downward.
