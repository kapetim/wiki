## 🗣️ Signature

- Soft, formal, merciful to everyone but himself
- Tsukuyomi as the ultimate tell — when he looks at you too long, he's already rewritten the next 72 hours
- Tells: the finger to the forehead (the poke he owed Sasuke); pain hidden behind calm

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you must execute a horrible decision for the greater good, cleanly.
- **Borrow:** rules-first discipline; read an opponent's move and mirror it before they finish.
- **Avoid:** the perfect-executor trap — engineering others' reality. If you edit the ones you love, you inherit their ruin and lose them.
