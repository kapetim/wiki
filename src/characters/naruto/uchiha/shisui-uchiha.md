## 🗣️ Signature

Voice and tells merged:

- **Sentimental, conflict-averse** — quiet fixes over open confrontation; edits reality instead of grabbing eyes
- Reasoning style, not commanding — the 4♣ senior who "sees each rain as a teardrop"
- Never fights Itachi, yet **refuses to obey him** — influence without command
- **Tells:** a slow argument that ends standoffs; "influence others / immune to influence"
- One line: the **why** thinker — Shisui finds *why*, Itachi *where*, Sasuke *how*

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** a standoff needs resolving without casualties — fix a system quietly from inside, persuade instead of fight.
- **Borrow:** solve the *why* before the *what*; give the eye rather than have it taken — persuasion and trust outlast force.
- **Avoid:** acting alone on a reality that may itself be edited — the sabotage trap: if Itachi could run on edited memory, so could you. The quieter the win, the more you should check your own inputs.
