## 🗣️ Signature

- Speech is clipped, cold — anger as an economy of words
- Sad-memory triggers flip him into revenge mode; the Mangekyō unlocks on loss
- Tells: the headband worn as a weapon; silence where warmth should be

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need single-minded pursuit against a system, or discipline through grief.
- **Borrow:** the eye-inheritance instinct as *drive* — turn loss into resolve.
- **Avoid:** the A♠ trap — isolation as virtue. Solo revenge collects allies you could have kept.
