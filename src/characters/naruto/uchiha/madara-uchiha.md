## 🗣️ Signature

- Speaks in absolutes — peace, power, the plan; no room for a second opinion
- Instant escalation: kill, harvest, move on
- Tells: treats every ally as a future puppet; the calm of someone who has outlived his enemies

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need founder-scale vision or to drive a long game through proxies.
- **Borrow:** the willingness to manage people from the shadows — delegation with a thesis.
- **Avoid:** the #1 trap — instant extremes and eye-harvesting the closest. The man who treats people as puppets ends the sole author of his own tragedy.
