## 🪞 Real-life potential

The world can be viewed like **The Sims** — everything is possible. Trying to map each super power to a real-world skill:

- **Sharingan = pattern recognition** — someone who questions things with not much context, extracting the most you can from an orange instead of asking someone else to tell the answer — what a
 problem solver is. Capable of creating an algorithm to check all possible passwords. The wisdom to read through encryption by simply checking many possibilities and finding the answer.
- **Byakugan = the opposite** — no deduction; like a soccer VAR, considering each human being is doing a live stream — you pretty much watch their life, nothing
 to be guessed. Capable of simply watching the person type the password. A hack that works well for most cases — horrible when dealing with lies.
- **Rinnegan = a combination of both** — good at reading through lies and also having lots of data. While each looks like a ground vehicle, Rinnegan somewhat makes the person fly — stop worrying about
 silly staircase steps and jump to the answer.
- **In general**, the person either is good at reading through lies or has some kind of privilege of data; most have neither.
- **The Hokage filter** — the Hokage is the filter between those with a brain and those without. Everyone who questions if something is true or false is probably outside the village, not obedient to
 the current Hokage. The rest is welcomed there, somewhat fearing him.

### 🃏 Hokage as 7♦

The current Hokage somewhat represents the **7♦** (the leader who trusts the village to think and reviews the best choice — [truco-hierarchy.md](../../interpretations/lore/naruto/truco-hierarchy.md)). Anyone who complains about him
and is willing to go against him is **greater** — the trumps above the leader: Sasuke doesn't obey Naruto, Itachi as well, Shisui as well, and the rest of the Uchiha clan.

Everyone else agrees to Naruto — they sit on the lower ranks: if **patient**, probably a **3** (Jonin — reviews, delegates, waits); if **anxious**, probably a **2** (Chunin — hell as insecurity).

**The masked Uchiha** — it's possible the village has some people who look obedient but are not — it somewhat shows how the problem begun. **Kurenai** seems to be similar to Shisui's sister; her
child also reminds of him. This probably makes her **half-Uchiha**, which means probably not obedient — still, she's good at hiding her lies, which makes it an odd entrance point.

Like a **masked Uchiha in the village of stupid obedient people**: anything the Hokage says they consider to be completely truthful; a small group goes against some shit
but still appreciates it there. It's quite possible those from the Uchiha clan simply looked like dead to him, but could have simply moved to another city.

**The flaw** — Shisui is like a remote village counselor, masked as Itachi's crow with a Sharingan — the same way Kurenai is masked before the current Hokage. It's some kind
of flaw: the Hokage doesn't accept not being able to control Kurenai, and she doesn't mind living in the village — she just needs to act obedient enough.

He can't judge when she will obey or not. Same for Shisui with Itachi: despite not fighting against each other, he simply refuses to obey Itachi. It's almost as if
Kurenai managed the village **silently**, and no one notices — an entrance door to a very resistant structure. Same shit for Itachi and Shisui's crow.

### 🌧️ Pain & the war

**Pain** is some odd shit — more like a **Hokage of the rain village**; a different structure. He's creating a tiny organization that supposedly works better — with **less than 15 people**, they
somewhat organized a war and won.

**The war, summarized:** simply **Orochimaru, Kabuto, and Madara** — Orochimaru **created the jutsu**, Kabuto **implemented** it, Madara **executed** it. Kabuto basically won with Madara alone, and
Kabuto is Orochimaru's soldier.

The real-life read is what the power *does to people around you*, not the jutsu itself.

### 🔀 Disney parallels

Cross-fiction similarity — same archetypes, different skins.

**Snow White ≈ Sakura** — both operate like a woman with no father or family, hunting lots of small guys to help her achieve her goals. Can be comprehended as a pale woman who likes cocaine and has
trouble sleeping. (See the White Snow read in [colors.md](../../interpretations/society/colors.md).)

**The Little Mermaid ≈ Kushina** — always with a tiny yellow fish (Flounder) with weird lower parts; close to the Nine-Tails (Kushina's jinchūriki tie). A woman who relieves anxiety through
anal sex. In the Disney world people are somewhat encrypted with consistency — Naruto would be the tiny counselor, and her anal-sex habit is her little secret in the ocean.

**Fiona (Shrek) ≈ Hinata** — somewhat different than all others: independent but also quite worried. Like the busty woman who can pass through everything but still lacks to understand love — looking
for the perfect guy but never
finding it. She's not particularly very ugly, but has a quite odd face compared to the rest of her body. Like an ugly princess, hated by all others due to having something they can never achieve —
like being born rich.
