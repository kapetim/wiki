# 🗂️ Control ladder — the village tiers

**What this is:** the Naruto village as a ranked control cast — who holds power, who obeys the Hokage, and
how the uncontrollable are contained. The generic owner→obedience ladder and the physical ceiling now live in
[monitoring.md](../../observation/monitoring.md) and [hard-limit.md](../../observation/fundamentals/hard-limit.md). **Metaphor only.**

## 📊 Control ranking

The village stacked top to bottom — **0** the operator above the sim, **6** the non-criminal baseline.

<!-- begin table -->
| # | Tier | Control | Read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | **Life-game owner** (≈ rain village) | Operator | Above the sim — sees everything, wants many distinct powerful citizens without collapse |
| **1** | **Tailed beasts** | Uncontrollable | The nine beasts plus Gedō Mazō — immune even to Uchiha genjutsu |
| **2** | **Uchiha** | Controllers | Keep the Hokage on the line — manage others with ease |
| **3** | **Byakugan** | Watchful | The Hyūga — VAR / live-stream watch, no deduction |
| **4** | **Naruto** | Trusted | The **7♦** mayor — owner of money, keeps the population safe and working |
| **5** | **Ninja** | Partial | Ranks 1/2/3 ≈ genin/chunin/jonin — the criminal band that avoided jail |
| **6** | **Non-ninja** | Full | Civilians — work and pay bills, no crime; inherited powers from ancestors |

## 🛡️ Containment and the hunt

Gedō Mazō also stands for the Hokage line's pursuit of ways to manage people who could cause trouble to the
village — if they ran wild, they could bring lots of trouble. Learning how to capture them is key to safety.
The **Akatsuki** hunts the beasts one by one — 1 → 9, strict rule order — showing other villages how to
handle things with mastery.
