## 🗣️ Signature

- Loud, drunk, brilliant — bravado hiding the healer's grief
- Bets everything on "lucky" — because the odds never favored her either
- Tells: freezes at blood in the wrong moment; heals the whole village before herself

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need the strength to lead after devastating loss.
- **Borrow:** invest everything in the next generation — bet on them like your life depends on it.
- **Avoid:** the grief-run trap — running from the throne after loss only delays the healing you could start.
