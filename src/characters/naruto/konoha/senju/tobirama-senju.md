## 🗣️ Signature

- Speaks in rulings, not feelings — the architect's register
- Favors speed and seals: get it done before the argument starts
- Tells: trusts loyal exceptions (Kagami) while legislating against their clan — the contradiction he never resolves

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need to build the system, not win the moment — institutions over charm.
- **Borrow:** founding rigor — write the rules so the village survives its founders.
- **Avoid:** policy-as-distrust. Systems built on excluding a group hand that group the grievance that eventually burns the system.
