## 🗣️ Signature

- The warm, booming laugh — a god of war who prefers flowers and naps
- Treats tailed beasts like pets to be shared
- Tells: the earnest grin before he does something impossibly powerful

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you're building the system and must trust your rival to help run it.
- **Borrow:** the founder's read — peace is a structure you build, not a truce you hope holds.
- **Avoid:** the optimism trap — giving away the weapons without a mechanism to keep them unused is how the peace gets broken.
