# 👮 Konoha police — the Uchiha clan force

**What this is:** the Leaf's police read — the **Uchiha clan** as the village's own enforcers, and why that became the
massacre's logic.

## 🏛️ The read

The Leaf's police = the **Uchiha clan** — a clan force put to **watch itself**, "more loyal to the crime faction than
to the government."

## 👮 The toll police

They catch the **small stuff** (petty crime) and never end the faction: cameras, internet, enough weapons — yet
nothing changes. Enforcement as a **toll**, not a resolution.

## 🔁 Clan vs itself

The Uchiha confront **themselves**, propagating lies that **Shisui** exposes by reasoning alone — competition of
control, him soloing both sides.

## 🧩 The crossing of data

Shisui's confusion + the police disappearing + **Kushina and Minato** dying to the Nine-Tails land at once; the Hokage
orders the massacre, and the Leaf runs **without police**.

## ⚔️ The order

The Hokage orders **Itachi** to kill them all — and that's it. The system the clan policed ordered its own removal.

## 🧭 Philosophy read

Enforcement keeps a ledger only while its keeper stays outside the crime — a clan force watching itself collapses the
layer ([fair-game.md § establishment](../../../observation/justice/fair-game.md)).
