## 🗣️ Signature

- Reads *Icha Icha* in public, says nothing, sees everything
- The senior replicator — copies any jutsu once he reads it, like Sasuke but senior
- Tells: the masked lower face; tardiness as a cover for never being late to what matters
- Voice: dry, understated — emotional weight delivered as deadpan

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you're teaching people to surpass you, or carrying borrowed tools you must honor.
- **Borrow:** rules as a starting point, team over rules when it counts.
- **Avoid:** the grief-armor trap — a mask and a book keep people out until you forget how to let them in.
