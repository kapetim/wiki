## 🪞 Appearance

Trait-level: pale-eyed and soft — the shy heiress whose gentle face hides the clan's fierce bloodline.

## 🗣️ Signature

- Speech trails into ellipses — hesitation given a voice
- "Byakugan!" — the activation that finally arrives when it counts
- Tells: finger twiddling when nervous; her gaze steadier than her words

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you see everything clearly but doubt yourself at the decisive second.
- **Borrow:** gentleness as a real strength — protect without crushing.
- **Avoid:** the hesitation trap — an eye that sees all is wasted if you look away when it matters.
