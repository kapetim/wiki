## 🪞 Appearance

Trait-level: long red hair and a temper to match — the Uzumaki fire that Naruto inherited as volume and Kushina wielded as will.

## 🗣️ Signature

- Loud, blunt, warm — "dattebane" energy before her son made it his own
- The chains that rise when the ones she loves are threatened
- Tells: scolds with a smile; fights like the village depends on it, because it does

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you want fierce, protective love with zero pretense.
- **Borrow:** the Uzumaki will — hold the line for your people even when it costs everything.
- **Avoid:** the sacrifice trap — a love that dies keeping every secret can orphan the child it was protecting.
