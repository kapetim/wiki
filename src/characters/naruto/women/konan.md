## 🗣️ Signature

- Paper-light presence — folds into the world, watches everything
- Collects every rumor, never sorts truth from lie — data as devotion
- Tells: the paper flowers she leaves behind; silence where judgment should be

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you want loyal observation — gather everything, decide nothing on your own.
- **Borrow:** fidelity to a person's dream, not to a system.
