## 🗣️ Signature

- Quiet, agreeable on the surface — the obedience that lets her run things unnoticed
- Genjutsu as her real register: reads what people believe before they say it
- Tells: never visibly disobeys, yet never actually obeys — the door in the resistant structure

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need to work inside a system that demands obedience without surrendering your judgment.
- **Borrow:** silent management — act obedient enough, keep the real veto.
- **Avoid:** the mask trap — a life of managed quiet means no one notices the entrance door, including you.
