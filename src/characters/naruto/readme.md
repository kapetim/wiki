## 🔥 Uchiha clan

A Leaf clan kept separate — its identity is the clan, not the village. The genius clan of the Leaf: **strong emotion awakens the Sharingan**, love lost turns it to Mangekyō. Two poles run
through the bloodline: the **copy line** (Tobirama → Kagami → Shisui → Itachi — village-first, persuasion over blood) and the **trauma line** (Madara → Obito → Sasuke — loss accelerates the
eye).

**The clan grammar:** sight is inherited by *taking* — EMS comes from a close kin's eyes; witnessed death is unreliable (genjutsu, body substitution, staged duels). The clan ends in the
**massacre** Itachi carried out — the system they policed ordering their own removal. Eye deep-dive: [sharingan.md](uchiha/sharingan.md).
