## 🔢 The DDD joke

The AK numbers are a private area-code (DDD) joke — **48 = Florianópolis**, **49 = Santa Catarina**. Shisui is
the one without a number: the middle point, a gas-station counselor who digests problems from both hell and
heaven, connecting the dots with ease but without enough strength to need his own gun.
