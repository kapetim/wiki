## 🗣️ Signature

- Speaks of pain like a law — "the world shall know pain"
- Six voices, one will — the center never shows itself
- Tells: the rain that never stops; ideology worn as armor

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you want the gravity of someone who has suffered and built a system from it.
- **Borrow:** turning grief into a coherent worldview that people can follow.
- **Avoid:** the pain-thesis trap — a system founded on shared suffering recruits, but it can't stop the next rain.
