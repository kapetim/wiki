## 🗣️ Signature

- Grumpy accountant's register — complains about costs while fighting
- Sews himself back together mid-battle — oddly capable of **refactoring his own limbs** with threads
- Tells: counts his hearts like money; treats partners as expenses

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you want the discipline of someone who prices everything and wastes nothing.
- **Borrow:** pragmatism — treat every effort as an investment.
- **Avoid:** the ledger trap — a life reduced to accounts has no reason left when the money's gone.
