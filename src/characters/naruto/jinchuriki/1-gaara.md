## 🪞 Appearance

Trait-level: the red-haired boy with the gourd and the raccoon eyes — menace that reads as *do not touch*, softened when he becomes the sand that shields.

## 🗣️ Signature

- Quiet, short, absolute — a boy who learned words could kill
- The sand speaks for him — defense as body language
- Tells: the "do I exist?" wound under every calm answer; sleep as the one vulnerability

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need to turn an isolation wound into protectiveness instead of destruction.
- **Borrow:** self-rescue through one honest mirror — someone seeing you changes the target.
- **Avoid:** the weapon trap — believing love only comes from being useful. It comes from being seen.
