## 🗣️ Signature

- "Believe it!" (dattebayo) — verbal proof he exists; loudness as armor against being ignored
- Talk-no-jutsu: wins the argument by *feeling* the opponent's loss first
- Never lies about a promise — his word is the only inheritance he had
- Tells: the grin that hides the orphan; gets loudest when hurt

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need to win someone over by understanding them, or to persist where acknowledgment is withheld.
- **Borrow:** recognition before correction — see the person before the problem.
- **Avoid:** the 7♦ trap — believing a reformed heart equals a reformed system; check whether the structure underneath changed.
