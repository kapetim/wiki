## 🗣️ Signature

- Slow, teasing drawl — a man who finds everything beneath him
- Licks his lips at new knowledge — curiosity with no ethics
- Tells: treats students as experiments, rivals as puzzles; never the true believer

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need a mind that refuses the frame — the outsider who sees the game itself.
- **Borrow:** relentless learning; question the rules instead of playing them.
- **Avoid:** the boredom trap — a curiosity with no loyalty makes you the devil of every story you touch.
