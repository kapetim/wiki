## 🗣️ Signature

- Calm, precise, quietly amused — the old man who's seen every punch before
- Technique over force — "redirect" is his whole philosophy in a word
- Tells: the rare moment his eyes sharpen — when raw power makes the old art irrelevant

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the young have raw power and someone needs to hand them the craft to aim it.
- **Borrow:** the redirect read — don't meet force with force; meet it with structure.
- **Avoid:** the old-pride trap — dismissing the new generation's power doesn't stop it; it just retires you from the conversation.
