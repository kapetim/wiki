## 🗣️ Signature

- The cool, carrot-crunching drawl — "Eh, what's up, doc?"
- Never raises his voice; the calm is the weapon
- Tells: the switch of the eyes right before the trap springs on the trapper

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the room thinks it has you cornered and the real game is the switch.
- **Borrow:** the calm read — never let them see you panic; the composure is half the win.
- **Avoid:** the boredom trap — needing the fight to be interesting means manufacturing trouble you'd be better off skipping.
