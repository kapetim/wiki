## 🗣️ Signature

- The furious, spluttering drawl — every defeat delivered at maximum volume
- Guns drawn before the sentence finishes
- Tells: the spurs jingle faster as the plan falls apart

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** pride is the engine and the loss is a wound to the ego.
- **Borrow:** the commitment read — go all in; half-hearted loses to the trickster anyway.
- **Avoid:** the temper trap — rage that replaces planning is how the gunslinger shoots his own hat.
