- The flat, reasonable monotone — "Where's the kaboom?"
- Doom announced with impeccable manners
- Tells: the tiny fury barely contained behind the visor

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** a measured, procedural approach meets a chaos agent.
- **Borrow:** the calm-threat read — menace doesn't need volume; it needs certainty.
- **Avoid:** the over-planning trap — perfect plans leave no room for the rabbit who doesn't read the manual.
