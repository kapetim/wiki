## 🗣️ Signature

- "Eat my shorts" / "Ay caramba!" / "I didn't do it" — the trinity of rebellion
- The troublemaker with a soft spot — pranks first, feelings a reluctant second
- Tells: the grin that means someone's about to get pranked

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** an institution needs a well-aimed thumb on the nose.
- **Borrow:** the trickster read — question authority with humor, not just volume.
- **Avoid:** the eternal-ten trap — rebellion without growth is a sitcom; with growth it's a revolution.
