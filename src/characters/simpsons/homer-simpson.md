## 🗣️ Signature

- "D'oh!" — the whole philosophy in one syllable
- Short-term desire with a heart — impulsive, loud, occasionally devastatingly sincere
- Tells: the sparkle in his eye right before the bad idea

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the room needs the everyman who says the dumb thing everyone's thinking.
- **Borrow:** the appetite read — know what people want now; short-term desire drives most of life.
- **Avoid:** the reset trap — incompetence is only lovable when love keeps paying the bill; in real life it compounds.
