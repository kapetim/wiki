## 🗣️ Signature

- The cheerful, wholesome register — "hi-diddly-ho, neighborino"
- Forgives before he's asked
- Tells: the quiet prayer before the next disaster hits

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** kindness is the whole strategy and the world keeps testing it.
- **Borrow:** the good-neighbor read — decency is not naivety; it's a decision renewed daily.
- **Avoid:** the doormat trap — forgiveness that never sets a boundary just invites the Homer next door to keep knocking.
