# ♿ Disability — unsolvable

**What this is:** a body change with **no definitive fix** — tissue does not regrow; the best is adaptation.

- **Limb loss** — no regrow; a prosthetic is a tool, not restoration.
- **Spine / nerve damage** — constant pain, possible loss of movement; may herniate.
- **Bad bone union** — the body stays different.
