# 🎲 Randomness — unverifiable RNG

**What this is:** an **unsolvable** problem — when the game and the auditor are the same party, "random" can hide anything.

- **League of Legends:** who controls how often a strong hit lands? Supposedly random — but hard-code a lower damage
  value and it is "fine". How do you sue **Riot Games** for **10 low-damage attacks in a row**? It is **mathematically possible**.
- **Lottery:** sabotage so you **never win** — *any* combination no one played satisfies the draw. Or **swap the ball
  weights** so a chosen combination comes up.
- The last check is a **group hired by the company** to confirm the company is not corrupt — which is no check at all.

- Can a player or a buyer ever prove a draw was rigged?
- What would a truly independent randomness auditor look like?
