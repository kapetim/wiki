# 👶 Parenting — whose child?

**What this is:** an **unsolvable** problem — a woman can gestate a child she did not conceive, and the one piece of paper that says otherwise can be faked.

## 🧬 Gestation vs genetics

- A woman can **carry and birth** an already-fertilized embryo — she becomes "the mother" by gestation, though the DNA is not hers.
- Genetics says otherwise, but whoever inserted it can also **fake the test** — **one piece of paper** "confirms" the result.
- Without an independent, incorruptible verifier there is **no certain answer**.

## ❓ Open questions

- Who is the mother — the carrier, the genetic source, or the one holding the paper?
- Can any test survive a corrupt lab?
