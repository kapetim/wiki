# 🏥 Healthcare — unsolvable

**What this is:** health conditions with **no definitive fix** — they never return to baseline; the best is management, not a cure.

## 🛑 Irrecoverable

- **HIV** — not curable; antiretroviral medication after exposure suppresses the virus and prevents progression.
- **Bone and nerve damage** — a lost limb does not regrow, and a broken bone at best stays where it is. Worst is
  nerve compression: constant pain, possible loss of movement; in the spine it may herniate.

## 🔮 Unknown

- **HPV** — skin warts that spread when immunity is low and may lead to cancer. A vaccine prevents many strains; the
  only known cure is good immunity letting the body heal itself.
