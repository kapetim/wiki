# 🎭 Accident — the masked assassin

**What this is:** an **unsolvable** problem — killing dressed as an accident. The act is the same; the **evidence** is what you hide.

- **Same act, different evidence:** shooting someone is an assassination; **frightening someone at the edge of a hill** so they step back and fall is *also* an assassination — dressed as bad luck.
- **Intent is provable only in a video game with one source of truth:** judgeable **only with logs** proving the person was frightened at the hill. Without the log, intent and accident are **indistinguishable**.
- **Direct violence is the same problem:** a standard violent method in an **unmonitored place** is equally unprovable — no witness, no record.

## ❓ Open questions

- Can intent ever be proven without a single source of truth?
- Who decides it was not bad luck?
