# ⚖️ Justice — the liked killer

**What this is:** an **unsolvable** problem — under corruption, reputation replaces evidence and the judge has to agree.

- A **serial killer everyone likes is fine** — the neighbourhood vouches for them.
- When it matters, witnesses **testify the assassin was a similar-looking guy**; the judge pretty much has to agree.
- Evidence is **social**: consensus replaces truth (see the [corruption premise](../readme.md#%EF%B8%8F-corruption)).

## ❓ Open questions

- If the crowd is the ledger, what is a fact?
- Who testifies against a liked killer?
