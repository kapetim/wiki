# 👥 Social — unsolvable

**What this is:** a social problem with **no definitive fix** — some exclusion does not lift; reputation is hard to rebuild.

- **Stigma** — a record, a disease, or a look can close doors for life.
- **Isolation** — the longer it lasts, the harder the re-entry ([mental-health.md](mental-health.md)).
- **The crowd as judge** — under corruption a liked killer is fine and the outsider is not ([justice.md](justice.md)).

## ❓ Open questions

- Can a reputation ever be reset, or only outlived?
- Who lets the excluded back in?
