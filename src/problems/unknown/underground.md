# 🕳️ Underground — the hidden settlement

**What this is:** an **unknown** problem — a place to disappear and live: no grid, no cameras, no wifi. What happens below is unknown until it is found.

## 🏕️ Living underground

- **No electricity, no cameras, no wifi** — candles, no grid.
- **Survive:** live below and never go out; eventually build a **power plant** — or **steal batteries**.
- **Secret aquarium:** full privacy, no comfort — at risk only if found.

## ⚙️ The wheel model

- **The disguise entry** — look-alike entrants, then the costume is dropped; no visual proof of who did it.
- **The hidden door** — leave through a tunnel, plaster the opening, rebuild the wall; the police lose days finding it.
- **The swap** — enter the metro, take a passenger's clothes, walk out looking like them.
- **The graph** — basements connect through paths; while a duplicate path exists, one can be blocked and the runner escapes.
- **The rabbit-hole race** — snakes (police) versus rabbits (criminals); the rabbits only need to hide faster than the police find them.

## 🏛️ The ownership turn

- Once enough crimes run through it, the government eventually finds and takes over the system.
- Police pick robust tools; criminals accept cheap tricks for one more night of sleep.

## ❓ Open questions

- How much of a city is genuinely unmonitored, and for how long?
- Does surveillance ever catch a path network, or only the people who surface?
- The only way in is monitoring life as a game — who owns the game?
