# 🏠 Privacy — the private box

**What this is:** an **unknown** problem — an apartment is private until it is not, and the way in is usually not the camera.

- **The apartment is the private box:** people generally cannot watch inside — not satellites, not neighbours — **as long as you close the windows**.
- **The complaint is sabotage:** devices, or the internet line.
- **Underground** you get **full privacy, no comfort** — a fish in a **secret aquarium**, at risk only if found; findable only by **monitoring life as a game**.
