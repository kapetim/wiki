# 📹 Surveillance — the deletable log

**What this is:** an **unknown** problem — things can be tracked, but the log is usually **deleted**, so no record is certain.

- **The standard game:** drive your car → maybe a street camera catches a ticket → then it is a game of complaining and fighting to hide it.
- It reads like a **2D game monitored from cameras on top** — the owner sees everything; the players see the map.
- Evidence is only as good as the disk that holds it ([corruption.md](corruption.md)).

## 🧠 Monitoring of thoughts

If **thoughts** can be read, then every plan is **already visible** — you can map **who the criminal is, when they are
going to act, and where they are and where they are going**. A crime is just a plan that has not been executed yet; a
reader of minds holds the verdict before the act.

## 🎮 Big Brother — the Pokémon-daycare loop

At best the watching is run as a **Big Brother over persons of interest** — a **Pokémon-daycare** of people:

- Put **choices together** and watch **how they react**.
- Once **bored**, **remove them one by one** — and justify the loop with a **prize for whoever survived longest**.

The prize is the **carrot that keeps the wheel spinning**; the elimination is the **entertainment**.

## 🏠 Life as The Sims

Taken to the end, life is **The Sims**: people live their lives and are eventually **kidnapped or influenced to join
Big Brother** — after that they are monitored **like Truman** while interacting with others.

- The stated reason is **entertainment masked as research**.
- The **only known escape is death**.

## ❓ Open questions

- If the log is deletable, what is a fact?
- If every thought is readable, is a crime even possible?
- Is death the only exit from the loop?
