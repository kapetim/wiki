# 🌐 Online — the unprovable identity

**What this is:** an **unknown** problem — online actions never come with a certain author.

- **Hard to track online** — people can **rewrite logs** and **bypass as others** (pretend to be someone else).
- **No proof of who is using the device** — the account is not the person.
- **No proof someone didn’t copy your network data** (MITM) and act as you.
- **The reverse is provable**: monitoring and **streaming a private space** *is* a known crime ([surveillance.md](surveillance.md), [privacy.md](privacy.md)) → psychological damage by spread.

## ❓ Open questions

- What proof ties an action to a person, not a device?
- Can a copied session ever be distinguished from the owner?
