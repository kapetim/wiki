# ♿ Disability — solvable

**What this is:** a body change with a **definitive fix** — rehab, prosthetics, and adaptation restore a working life.

- **Clean broken bone** — heals, often near-baseline.
- **Lost limb** — a prosthetic plus training restores function (adaptation, not regrowth).
