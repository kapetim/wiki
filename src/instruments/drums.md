# 🥁 Drums mapping

Rhythm-first translation of songs from melodic instruments.

## 👀 What to pay attention to when playing

Track these while practicing. Kit setup/tuning is easy; **limb independence and rhythm** are the ones to master.

- **Left arm** — hi-hat and snare work.
- **Right arm** — snare and ride work.
- **Left foot** — hi-hat pedal / clutch.
- **Right foot** — kick drum.
- **Limb independence** — coordinating all four while keeping the groove — the hard one.
- **Stickings** — the different ways of striking: `RLRL`, `RLLR`, paradiddles.
- **Dynamics** — ghost notes and accents.
- **Rhythm** — pulse, subdivision, feel — fundamental.

## 💡 Core idea

- Drums do not map note letters directly like strings/keys/winds.
- Map song sections by pulse, accents, and phrase lengths.
- Keep the same part structure (`A / B / C`) used in other instruments.

## 🎵 Notation rule

- Numbered count grid: `1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &`.
- Piece labels: section names + dynamic notes (`soft`, `accent`, `fill`).

## 🎵 Example song: Song of Storms (multi-part practice)

Reference sections shared with other instruments:

- Part A (8 notes)
- Part B (8 notes)
- Part C (8 notes)

Simple groove map:

```text
HH|x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-|
SD|----o-------o---|
BD|o-----o-o-------|
 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &
```

Practice transfer:

1. Play Part A groove 2x.
2. Add a short fill into Part B.
3. Keep same bar count into Part C.
4. Match accents with melody peaks from other instruments.

## 🎵 Full-song arrangement template

```text
Tempo:
Time signature:

Intro groove:
Part A groove:
Part A repeat:
Part B groove:
Part B repeat:
Part C / variation:
Bridge pattern:
Final loop:
Outro hit/decay:
```

Checklist:

1. Keep one groove/fill pattern per section label.
2. Mark section transitions with fill length (`1/2 bar`, `1 bar`).
3. Keep form labels synced with melodic instruments.
