# 🎹 Piano mapping

Reference layout for translating string-based songs to keys.

## 👀 What to pay attention to when playing

Track these while practicing. The sound/patch is easy to set; **hand independence and rhythm** are the ones to master.

- **Right hand** — melody and lead lines.
- **Left hand** — bass and comping.
- **Sustain pedal** — clean overlaps.
- **Articulation** — legato and staccato touch.
- **Dynamics / touch** — control across the keys.
- **Voicings** — chord spread and register.
- **Hand independence** — melody + comping at once — the hard one.
- **Rhythm** — timing and groove — the hard one.

## 🗺️ Core layout

- 12 semitones per octave.
- White-key natural letters: `A B C D E F G` repeating.
- Natural 1-semitone pairs: `B-C` and `E-F`.

## 🎵 Notation rule (always use both)

- Numbered pattern: scale degree numbers (`1 2 3 4 5 6 7`) or interval counts.
- Letter/chord label: `C`, `Am`, `F`, `G`, etc.

Example in `C` major:

- Degree pattern: `1 2 3 5`
- Letter notes: `C D E G`

## 🎵 Playing same song across instruments

1. Keep chord progression letters fixed.
2. Map each chord to left-hand root + right-hand triad.
3. Keep melody as degree pattern, then convert to letters.
4. Match phrase lengths with guitar/ukulele versions.

Progression example:

- Song chords: `Am - F - C - G`
- Keyboard voicing anchor: LH roots `A F C G`, RH triads with same chord letters.

## 🎵 Example song: Song of Storms (multi-part practice)

- Part A letters: `D F D F D F E C`
- Part B letters: `D F A G F E D C`
- Part C letters: `A C A C A C G E`
- Degree view in D natural minor:
  - Part A: `1 b3 1 b3 1 b3 2 b7`
  - Part B: `1 b3 5 4 b3 2 1 b7`
  - Part C: `5 b7 5 b7 5 b7 4 2`
- Chord context (loop): `Dm - C - Bb - A`

Right-hand melody (numbers and letters):

- Part A: `1 b3 1 b3 1 b3 2 b7` -> `D F D F D F E C`
- Part B: `1 b3 5 4 b3 2 1 b7` -> `D F A G F E D C`
- Part C: `5 b7 5 b7 5 b7 4 2` -> `A C A C A C G E`

Left-hand chord roots (one per bar):

- `D -> C -> Bb -> A`

This keeps the same harmonic letters used on guitar/ukulele/bass.

Checklist:

1. For each section, keep RH melody as degree numbers + letters.
2. Keep LH roots/chords aligned to the same section labels used by other instruments.
3. Mark pedal and voicing changes per section.
