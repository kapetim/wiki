# 🍽️ Diet rules

The rules; the amounts live in [food/diet](../food/diet/status.md).

- **Detox juice on wake** — with the supplement pills and vitamins; the morning energy covers the taste (at night it is heavy and unpleasant).
- **No cooking oil** — most food already has enough fat; olive oil goes on pasta only after cooking, as seasoning.
- **Bean soup includes meat** — pork, liver, or sardine; precook the meat under pressure, then recook with the daily beans.
- **Pasta is simpler, vegan** — drop the meat and add tomatoes.
- **Ranges are locked** — adjust the diet, never the L/R ranges ([status](../food/diet/status.md)).
