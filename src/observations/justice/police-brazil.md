## ⏱️ Prescrição (CP art. 109)

<!-- begin table -->
| Pena máxima | Prescreve em |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| ≤ 1y | 3y |
| 1 – 2y | 4y |
| 2 – 4y | 8y |
| 4 – 8y | 12y |
| 8 – 12y | 16y |
| > 12y | 20y |
<!-- end table -->

Starts counting from the crime date (or sentence date, after conviction). Interrupted by: denúncia, sentença
condenatória recorrível, acórdão confirmatório. After interruption, clock restarts from zero.

If the person is under 21 or over 70 at the crime date, all prescription periods are halved (CP art. 115).

obrigatoriedade de regime inicial fechado, mas na prática costuma ser fechado.

## 🤝 Resolving without prison

### 💰 Pay before the victim reports

Nothing happens. No case, no record, no process. The crime exists legally but nobody prosecutes because there's
nothing left to compensate. Best possible outcome.

### 💰 Pay at the police station (before anything formal)

If the victim shows up and you pay immediately — still no case. Delegado may record as "resolved" and archive it.
Especially works for: dano, furto simples, lesão leve.

### ⚖️ Composição civil (Lei 9.099/95 art. 74)

For crimes with max penalty ≤ 2y. Victim accepts compensation → case extinguished. No criminal record. Only
available in JECRIM (Juizado Especial Criminal).

Includes: dano, furto simples, lesão corporal leve, cárcere privado simples, ameaça, vias de fato.

### ⚖️ Transação penal (Lei 9.099/95 art. 76)

For crimes with min penalty ≤ 2y and non-violent. Prosecutor offers: community service + fine. Accept → no prison.
No criminal record (for most purposes). Reject → case proceeds normally.

### ⚖️ Suspensão condicional (Lei 9.099/95)

For crimes with min penalty ≤ 1y. Process paused 2–4y. Conditions: repair damage, no new crimes, periodic
reporting. If met → case dismissed at the end.

### ⚖️ Arrependimento posterior (CP art. 16)

If you repair damage or return property before the sentence: penalty reduced by 1/3 to 2/3. Doesn't kill the case
but substantially reduces the outcome.

### 🤔 What this means

If someone claims you broke their thing or owe them money: **pay it quickly. Before escalation.** If the damage is
undone, the crime goes with it. Only cases that survive compensation are violent crimes and trafficking — those have
a public interest that can't be settled privately.

### 🚫 Civil debt is not prison

Brazil does not imprison for unpaid civil debts. Only pensão alimentícia can lead to prisão civil. Everything else
(bills, loans, damages) is collection or credit restriction — not jail.

## 🙉 Ignore the noise

Ignore. Don't search. Don't worry. Move on.

**Jusbrasil / public records:**

- Some people check Jusbrasil, SERASA, or public court records as a "precaution."
- If there's a real problem, it will find you. Searching won't prevent it — it only causes mental distress and
  wasted time.
- A civil lawsuit or a protest (protesto de título) that you don't know about yet: you'll learn when it matters.
  Checking daily doesn't change the outcome.
- If someone searched your name and got scared of some random action — that's their problem, not yours.

**What a real problem looks like:**

1. **Credit restriction (nome sujo)** — you can't get credit, open accounts, or finance things. You'll know because
   you'll try to use credit and fail.
2. **Arrest warrant (mandado de prisão)** — police show up. Hard to miss.

Anything else is noise. Including:

- Random protest entries for small amounts (often expired or paid but not removed).
- Lawsuits from people who don't know who they're suing (wrong person, wrong address, wrong name).
- "Consultas" by curious civilians.

**Self-defense:** don't carry anything for it. A canivete won't help if they come from behind, have a gun, or catch
you off guard. If you didn't start the fight, you'll probably lose. Best defense is not being there.

**Rule:** never search your own name on legal sites. If something is wrong, it will reach you through official
channels (citation, arrest, credit block). Everything else is a waste of attention.

## 🧠 Personal choice — deliberate ignorance

- Prefer **not to be aware** of what is happening with my name (registries, new processes, lawsuits).
- Even knowing the risk, it buys freedom of mind. Being aware of new processes doesn't help anything.
- If a problem becomes real, the outcome is binary anyway:
  - **Prison** — imposed on you; no choice involved.
  - **Bill to pay** — an unemployed person can't pay it; if happily employed later, the situation may change.
- Crimes that carry prison rarely have an alternative. A lawyer can at best reduce the penalty time. Claiming
  anything more is a lie — and I'm not sure a lawyer can even protect against that.
