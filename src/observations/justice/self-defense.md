# 🛡️ Self-defense — the unprovable claim

**What this is:** self-defense as the judgement system's blind spot — pushed far enough, a person attacks to survive;
and with no proof, anyone can claim it for any act ([fair-game.md](fair-game.md)).

- **Survival first** — threatened enough, a person is **prone to attack to survive**; the instinct is not the crime.
- **Claim anything** — with **no proof** of what happened, any action can be called **self-defense**: an **infinite
  set of possibilities** in an **unmonitored** environment.
- **Cameras are finite** — a place has a **limited number of cameras**; the act happens **regardless** of them.
- **Responsibility = the proof against you** — being responsible depends heavily on **what people can prove**.
- **The house is the black box** — what happens **inside a person's home** is nearly impossible to prove; anything
  there is **unproven**.
- **Acting beats evidence** — a simple **acting session** can manufacture **fake proof**; performance becomes
  testimony.

## ❓ Open questions

- Without proof, how is self-defense ever disproved?
- Who is responsible when the only witness is an actor?
