## 🔤 Shared grammar

1. Emit **irritating cues** — noise, smirk, theft, prank
2. World **punishes** — chase, trap, humiliation
3. **Escape** — often impossible in real physics; reset next short
4. **Never surrender** — dignity through evasion, not victory

**Question:** causing chaos or attracting it? **Both** — personality draws trouble; trouble trains escape craft.

Real life has **no toon reset** — audience enjoys immunity fiction does not grant.

## 🎭 Looney vs trio

Looney is the **ensemble chase lane** (Bugs, Daffy, Road Runner). Felix / Woody / Pink Panther share archetype but **solo** character focus — keep separate hubs, link here.
