## ⛽ Shisui — the gas station

Shisui is potentially an unknown — **no DDD**. A middle point between hell and heaven, or simply a gas-station guy: someone who can digest most problems from hell and heaven, connecting the dots with
ease, but without enough strength to have its
own DDD or gun. Still, he collects some 50-cent coins here and there after hard work helping those with questions — giving a proper answer to someone crossing a bridge: what can be found, what

## ☯️ Karma and the life game

- **If you kill, you will probably have problems** — get paranoid, never enjoy life the same.
- Once you are a killer, the best is to **find the best routine**; better than that is to **not need it but have courage** whenever needed.
- If it were about assassination in the life game, no one would work. People craft some balance and sometimes seek guidance for cases when they forget about consequences.
- **Every action triggers karma through vengeance.** A good action attracts good things; a bad action attracts bad things. Not perfect — may take too long or not.
- Being too anxious to get something may make you a **winner for a day**, then depressed afterwards.
- When you beat the life game you are welcomed to **suicide** — out of ideas of what to do. Still, lots of tricks help people find a will to continue alive.
- Those who crossed that border tend to be **achievement-list creators** — defining interesting things to do in life to keep the wheel spinning. Go beyond car decoration: focus on **why** drive the
  car, **where** to go, **how** to get it done.

## 🎮 Simple game principles

- Everyone joins the game to **win or spend their time** — somewhat trying to get attention by being good at something.
- Most of the time a **loser crafts lots of excuses to sabotage others**. Regardless of their method, reasoning, and intent, it is a **bad action**.
- If you have a camera, the footage gets **stored somewhere**. If you burn all evidence, you lost the original footage — you are left with some ideas.
- A game is a game: **play your best move and deal with the consequences**.
- By winning all matches you risk **exposure from accumulated hatred**; those who are not so good start to pursue you out of lack of intelligence. Lots of praise — still, a single person can go out in
  the streets and kill you for being sentimental.
- **There is no guarantee in the life game.** Play your best move considering the strategy you appreciate the most.

## 🎭 The triad — why / where / how

Shisui is responsible for finding **why**; Itachi for defining **where**; Sasuke for discovering **how**. Example: you must go to the grocery store or you die of hunger — once per week. Itachi finds
the best store, best price, less effort;
Sasuke defines if you go running or walking, listening to music. Naruto collects the combination and sells it to the rest of the village — hiding names and propagating the best routine so everyone has
a minimum standard of life.

## ♾️ The infinite Uchiha war

- Once Itachi finds it impossible to keep alive while the fight continues, he makes the same movement Shisui made — unlocking a Nintendo of a friend once they get too bored with their traditional

## 🐦⬛ The crow

Shisui influenced Itachi to believe he is dead so Itachi could consider himself the best of the world — the crow. That was enough to make him believe he is free and super important, helping with
marriage
recommendations and controlling Sasuke to keep him from regretful actions. Naruto stimulated him to keep improving — always up to date with Sasuke, always seeking something above to justify his place.
