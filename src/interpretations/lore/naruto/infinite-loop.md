# ♻️ Infinite loop

**What this is:** the loop machinery — leaders who get replaced by the same thing, movies that restart, and the karma that keeps the wheel spinning. Pairs with [orchestrator.md](./orchestrator.md)
(the village manager), [power-guns.md](./power-guns.md) (reaction styles), and [anko-server.md](./anko-server.md) (the human conclusion). **Metaphor only.**

## 🐍 Removing Voldemort

What happens once you remove Voldemort? Hell continues, but with a new leader. Considering some patterns, it seems like it always shrinks to the same thing: you lost time trying to change the name of
the responsible while not being able to
notice any difference — pretty much some sentimental shit to destroy Voldemort and put Lucius in power. Perhaps Lucius is a younger version of Voldemort — somewhat less powerful, but maybe more
recommended for the new standards.

Voldemort was too old and would eventually die; if they can revive him, then it is not very likely that he would have such problems. So, changing the leader seems like a daydream.

## 🏘️ Swapping the mayor

In the village, no one can be revived or live forever — so you have to keep swapping the mayor while maintaining similar structures. It's like defining law and standards: you just replace the current
person to blame.

## 🎬 The 8-movie loop

Harry Potter shows a world in which you can revive Voldemort but still chooses to kill him — quite odd that it never had a sequence. Maybe he would get revived again and run the
same loop, as if the next movie were simply a restart from the first one. Voldemort killed Harry, then people tried to make Voldemort die, then everyone made a baby.

Why would they never revive Voldemort again? What prevents Harry from being killed? It is pretty much an infinite loop: after movie 7 part 2 you go back to movie 1. Kill
Harry and his spouse, then the new child becomes the new Harry in a new movie 1 — an infinite loop of 8 movies. After the last one it repeats the same story.

People from Hogwarts seem to keep clapping to this — like watching an infinite-loop show where some details change here and there. If you know who is the child of Harry Potter, it
is very likely this person is going to have a very similar life. All other parents who saw their kid lose to Harry are going to irritate him throughout the loop.

You go to the cinema and want to be entertained; people watch this Hogwarts show trying to discover who is who. After the end, everyone must have a baby and live life — which is like restarting from
movie 1. They may decorate the
castle differently, but they keep the same houses. Umbridge keeps trying to control Harry without success; people keep trying to win against Harry and then complain about humiliation in public after
such loss.

Still, they can't seem to realize this infinite loop — once they do, it probably gets boring. They make a baby and keep the wheel spinning.

If you kill Voldemort you are suggesting a world without Slytherin, which is like going back in time. If you keep the same structure, Slytherin promotes assassination: anyone angry at Harry could
simply kill him and repeat the same story
again. There is an orphan child seeking some attention — evaluate this as some Itachi shit: Harry as Sasuke trapped into some repetitive shit. Shisui somewhat convinced Itachi to trap Sasuke due to

## ⚖️ Karma of killing

Life is a game, but reasonably managed. Anyone can kill, but then you must have courage. What happens after killing? You need vengeance.

- **Karma is black&white** — old people watching events and judging them. Someone killed, so they must pay; define rules and stick to them.
- A crime may happen without being noticed, or people may charge it wrongly. See a crime but not be sure who the culprit was — two similar people can be confused. What happens if you kill the wrong
  person? It starts a **domino effect of shit** — you must kill the right person.
- Depending on the point of view, there is an end: if everyone agrees, they can eliminate a single person. Still, the same problem may appear again — an irritated person who kills another without good
  reasoning, which triggers the vengeance loop again.
- Receive news of a death → investigate who is responsible → seek guidance of what to do → put someone to go after → review the results.

How come Voldemort is revived once and never again? You are killing the leader of Slytherin due to some sentimental crap — what is the karma from such action? Kill the responsible for such
shit: killing Voldemort costs having to craft a baby and die right after — whatever die means, like Sasuke leaving the village while Sarada is raised without a father.

It is an infinite sadness shit. The village promotes self-defense and discovers how to eliminate a target, just never thought of the consequences.

## 🎮 If life is a game

If life is a game, everything is possible. Simply collect the metadata of a Hokage and you can spawn as many instances as you need. Keeping it to just one, with some rules, makes
people believe more into it — still, it creates trouble: people join some infinite competition trying to say their older friends would beat everyone, and no one seems to.

**Traceability** — if life is not a game, you pretty much can commit a crime without being caught: kill someone in the underground, no internet connection, no wifi, no mobile data, just plain eyesight
— how is that
not traceable? If life is a game, you pretty much saw a video of one sim killing another sim in the Sims — the game itself is the record. The two framings flip whether a crime is traceable at all.

## 👁️ Sharingan overpowered

The Sharingan is somewhat the most overpowered thing: you copy their powers, so you pretty much keep waiting for any new power and replicate the behavior. You would need to kill the person
while sleeping, which is not what happens in general. It is like the village being envious of those who have the Sharingan and discovering their best strategy in a short time.

If you are a human, you learned it; if a human can learn, they also can. If life is a game, you can simply put a very efficient machine to copy as quickly as possible. In the meantime between an
attack and it reaching the target, the person can craft a defense and ignore the attack.

## 📡 The live stream

If it is not a game, then things don't have a good meaning. A live stream somewhat guarantees some video-game shit — so it is possible to simply have a very efficient machine that can handle most
stuff. It is like having a phone
and ignoring the calls, removing the chip or so. From time to time people get a bit cocky due to being fooled for long, and then face some consequences — like if the game maintainers were just
watching over it.

## 🪙 The karma coin

You played fair and were treated with unfairness, so you pretty much get a coin to pursue vengeance. If you are too lazy, this coin may be granted to someone
else — so you can pretty much just chill and never bother about shit. But then life is too boring: keep pursuing good moments while avoiding mistakes.

Everything you do has a cost, and the earliest you pay, the less you suffer. Sometimes postponing a payment to a day before death is better than living in jail — like committing suicide when you know
you have terminal cancer: you go on a killing rampage one day before. People will have to create a next life just to say you are in a reborn jail paying for the damage you caused.

## 🎬 Truman — the psychological boss

Perhaps Truman is paying for some previous-life sentimental damage — made too many people sad and had to be heavily monitored. If he gets smart, it's about considering life as a live stream with no
cost.
AFAIK, telepathy and visual streaming without consent is not considered a law yet — like committing a crime before having a law. You can spread hatred by thoughts without having to pay for that.

The only way to fix it is by telling people life is a video game and that your brain and eyesight are monitored; while not formalizing such rules, it is simply impossible to do any shit.

The person is living their life and others are simply complaining about it — like pooping and being accused of appearing naked in a live stream. That is a problem for the person
who decided to stream their privacy; until they find a way to stop pooping, the person must get naked. They can also ignore such speeches and live their life.

Truman shows the trauma of realizing life is a monitored game. They tried to say the person is seen by cameras — still, a single flight can solve it. If you did a
live stream with your phone turned off in the middle of the desert, the only way is if someone else is monitoring you by some satellite shit.

In an apartment, people can sabotage by putting cameras everywhere; but if you keep running a live stream in the middle of the desert, or some bunker room close to a volcano, it is quite likely life
is
a video game. As long as you don't commit suicide, Truman has some kind of infinite attack through telepathy — funny, but kinda irrelevant: a representation of one psychological boss.

After that, you can continue life trying to understand more details of the game and suggesting improvements so that things go better.

## 🤖 You are a bot

If they spent so much time coding this shit and keeps streaming, it is probably interesting and well managed. Still, you are pretty much like a bot in a video game — not like you can run away from
your problems. They can turn off the computer without a
warning, or simply force you into an uncomfortable situation. The best is to assume eventual karma remains: allow people to live and keep notes of their behaviors. It tends to attract a championship,
but it is very hard to maintain.

If life is a game, anyone can be receiving some private information through odd methods — like putting a specific bot in the game to perform better than others simply for the sake of doing it.

## 🌸 Sakura — the karma protector

Sakura somewhat stimulated the village to keep this wheel spinning; after so much trouble, they must give her some shit to worry about and stop making mess. She is like the karma protector, which
makes a
person keep seeking vengeance. Still, by killing Voldemort you must provide a new life — then it is your responsibility to keep this life at all costs; otherwise, Voldemort will get back.

**Parenthood makes the difference** — Sakura went from a random girl to the next Uchiha mother: promoted from stupid to smart. Still, with great powers, great responsibility — having to deal with a
kid with a
back problem and no obedience. The smartest and also most hated girl. Sasuke may be just fine, enjoying some kind of retirement while Sakura starts paying for her mistakes by raising a kid alone.

### 🔥 The independent woman

Sakura is the independent woman who loves to have sex — her goal was never a prince, but a baby on her own terms. Her options came down to two: stick to some plastic shit (a safe, artificial partner
who looks good but has nothing real behind it), or be with a
revived person — Sasuke, the one who came back from the dead and then left again. She chose the revived one: a man who owes her nothing and won't stick around to help, which keeps her independent,
angry, and free to have the baby her way.

### 🤰 The baby goal — single-mother kekkei genkai

**Her real kekkei genkai is being a single mother angry about everything** — a bloodline limit of one, inherited by no one, lived alone. The major goal is that she must have a baby: she sustains the
village with reproduction
propaganda, stimulating everyone else to follow the same path. Otherwise most people would just compete against her for how long they can stay without a baby — and if people don't have children, the
life game stops.

When she has hers, it's a domino effect — everyone else follows. When an important person has a baby, the rest copy her, and that triggers the new generation loop (see
[the 8-movie loop](#-the-8-movie-loop)). By killing Voldemort you must provide a new life (the karma of killing); Sakura provides the new generation and keeps the wheel spinning — the smartest and
most hated woman in the village for it.

### 👁️ Kurenai — the early adopter

Kurenai was somewhat anxious and ended up having hers a bit earlier. In exchange she guaranteed some privacy and authority in the village — stepping away from

**Appearance over power** — Kurenai tends to be needy for being the best but rarely is. Having the baby first doesn't mean she's the best human alive; she is just a representation of appearance
and trustiness. She's not willing to trade her life for more power and performance. Kurenai probably wants to impress the Hokage, while Sakura wants to impress the other ninjas.

### 🎮 Mirai vs Sarada — the console read

The comparison of children: **Mirai is the original Nintendo console; Sarada is the portable computer with an emulator.**

- **Mirai** — born with the instinct to make the best choice; no unlocked shit, original cartridge, original device. She plays without understanding anything and still shows skill. A living propaganda
  for Nintendo: the
expensive latest device that only works if you buy from them. People trust the original because a console is made to be simply played — harder to hack than a desktop, so the gameplay is easier to
trust.
- **Sarada** — the effort perfection. She spent her time studying how to become better than Mirai — installed an emulator, bought a controller, configured everything. Tricky
  to set up, but it works, with better graphics and more performance. Like Dark Souls on PC vs console — highest settings, more FPS. Still, it's not original.

Some people are autistic and value only those who play on the original console — easier to trust the person's gameplay. It's easier to hack a desktop than a gaming console; while both are computers,
one was made to be configured and the other to be simply played.

**Anime character vs old-era novelty** — Sarada played a character in an anime while Mirai played some novelty of the old era.

**Herbivore vs carnivore** — Mirai is an herbivore wearing a carnivore ninja costume; Sarada is a natural carnivore trying to win all duels. The herbivores play an important role: they preserve
fairness and
keep their ass out of line during battles. Still, the carnivores tend to win all duels — like trying to use words against a gun, sad but true. Mirai doesn't fight; Sarada wins all duels.
Cross-refs: [power-guns.md](./power-guns.md) (Sakura the `.12`, Sarada the `AK-47`) and [odd-people.md](./odd-people.md) (the herbivore show).

### 🏗️ The reproduction machinery

To maintain the village, people need to have children — it makes sense. Every unsolicited child tends to go to the orphanage, which is not very helpful. Forcing someone to be a parent makes them raise
a piece of garbage.
It's better to put people to have kids with whoever they want — this way there's some guarantee that things will work well for the next 20 years. After that, the parent retires and starts to review
shit.

They all kept some kind of competition saying no one would have a baby. But after meeting Madara, they basically got put into a genjutsu and everyone suddenly became a parent — the domino made real by
an external trigger ([Madara — the all-in-one](#-madara--the-all-in-one)). The winner of the war opened an orphanage in the village; his mentor put a kid into the village, and life continued normally.
Cross-refs: [hell-heaven-bridge.md](./hell-heaven-bridge.md) (the war and the villages) and [odd-people.md](./odd-people.md) (Kabuto and the orphanage as Sarada's helper).
