# 🔒 Security — assume you can always lose

**What this is:** layered defense is worth building, but **every layer has a break** — perfect health can be attacked, perfect storage can be coerced open, infinite backups die to a gun to the head.
The honest stance is **you can always lose**: margins reduce the tail, they don't delete it.

Plus the reminder that the world is **not controlled** — police and guns exist because harm is real ([enforcement paradox](../perception/monitoring.md#-enforcement-paradox)). Pairs with
[hard-limit.md](./hard-limit.md) (prevention-only levers) and [fear-triage.md](./fear-triage.md) (don't rehearse the unpreventable). **Metaphor only.**

---

## 🪜 The stack

Defense-in-depth, best to worst: each inner layer depends on the outer ones staying intact.

<!-- begin table -->
| Layer | Protects | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Health** | Your body itself | Accident and organ failure still end the run ([hard-limit.md](./hard-limit.md)) |
| **Body** | Your person | Perfect health is still attackable by someone else |
| **Storage** | Your stuff | Perfect safe = you know the combo, so coercion can open it |
| **Secrets** | Your access | Passwords, recovery codes, biometrics — all extortable |
| **Backups** | Your data | Infinite copies die to a single forced handover |
| **Memory** | The last copy | In your head — only recoverable if you're freed to type again |
<!-- end table -->

---

## 💥 Every layer breaks

<!-- begin table -->
| Layer | How it breaks | What survives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Perfect health** | Attacked by someone else | Not much — body risk |
| **Perfect storage** | You know how to open it; coercion works | Nothing it holds |
| **Infinite backups** | Gun to your head, you share the password | Memorized facts |
| **Cameras** | Criminal locks you in a chair, removes limbs, uses your face | Still your face = your phone |
| **Your face** | Someone notices it and tracks your location | Nothing — it's always with you |
| **The chase** | VPN + an efficient car beats any camera net | The rat-and-mice race itself |
<!-- end table -->

## 🔫 Coercion beats crypto

No technical key survives **physical control** of the person holding it.

- Backups don't help when someone **points a gun and makes you share the password**.
- Biometrics are worse: your **face is the last key and it's always on you** — it unlocks the phone, which opens the online stuff, which leaks the location.
- Worst case: you're sedated in a bed, and **there's no execution from your side**. People can kill, sedate indefinitely, and torture as long as you survive.
- The hard target is the **person**, not the object — kidnapping the owner beats stealing the card ([monitoring § the person, not the object](../perception/monitoring.md#-enforcement-paradox)).

---

## 🐀 Rat-and-mice race

Cameras, logs, and monitoring slow a criminal down — they don't stop a smart one.

<!-- begin table -->
| Defense | Attacker's move |
| --- | --- |
| Cameras everywhere | VPN, masks, unmonitored route |
| Police on patrol | Efficient car, no traffic stops |
| Tracking your location | Move once, then vanish from the grid |
| Deterrence | Still wins when the prize justifies the risk |
<!-- end table -->

Result: **deterrence, not victory.** You make yourself expensive to hit; you don't make yourself unhittable.

---

## 🧠 Backups & memory

- **Good tools + backups** mean one mistake costs time, not total loss — single failure ≠ catastrophe.
- But backups assume **access**. If enough people want it gone, they can make your data disappear everywhere — and there's nothing you can do.
- **Memorization** is the last-resort copy: survive, get freed, type again.

---

## 🛏️ The agency ceiling

Plan for the states where you still have agency, not the ones where you don't.

- By the time you're **sedated** or **restrained**, no plan of yours executes.
- A "hack" like voluntary exit is unreliable precisely because **agency disappears first** — limbs lost, bed-bound, drugged. It's not a plan; it's a hope.
- Keep the [mood ≠ forecast](../fundamentals/fear-triage.md#%EF%B8%8F-mood--forecast) stance: horror fantasies are not forecasts, and there is usually another move before that point.

## 🎭 Reminders — the world is not controlled

**Movie vs video game:** if everyone were controlled, life would be a **movie** — a Disney film, everything working as expected, you just watch. If it's not a movie, it's a **video game**: anyone can
do anything, including to you. There's no controlled middle ground.

- Movies and news can all be **generated or edited** — but police exist and guns are real. If there were no crime, there would be no police.
- **Accept that harm exists** in this world — no one knows who will suffer and why.
- If an individual is free to torture anyone, who is the target? **If they were controlled, there would be no torture and no police.** Their existence means harm is real.
- **A live stream isn't bulletproof** — people would basically watch you dying during the stream.
- **Everyone has free will, each in their own way.** People make mistakes and are hurt by others — move on.
- Some people become officers to pursue those who make lolita victims — but that itself proves **there is no control**.
- If the system were perfect, there'd be no need for guns — people would simply know who is going to kill and **pause them before it**. Anything else means nobody knows who does what: you hear news,
  you hunt the criminal — but **who is the criminal, and who was the victim?**
- ~**1 police officer per 10k civilians** — virtually impossible to catch all criminals. Serial killers, school attacks, the Twin Towers: these aren't accidents — people
  **crafted a vengeance plan and executed it**.
- Police then run a **scavenger loop** to discover the reason and put a price on the actions — but those who suffered had already suffered.
- **Eventual karma:** everyone is allowed to do anything, but they're limited by **their fear of the consequences**.

---

## 🎯 What "assume you can always lose" means

- **Margins over certainty.** Post one protective margin, don't grind +1 in rumination ([harm § the loop](./harm.md#-the-loop--assess--post--reconcile)).
- **Prevention is the only lever** for the irreversible tier ([hard-limit § infinite tier](./hard-limit.md#%EF%B8%8F-the-infinite-tier--prevention-only)).
- **Don't rehearse the unpreventable.** Accident, coercion, and determined attackers are the irreducible tail ([fear-triage § "is further thinking
  useful?"](./fear-triage.md#-is-further-thinking-useful--template)).
- A good set of security tools is worth having — **but weight them by what is more likely to work**, not by what feels most absolute.
