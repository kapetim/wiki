# ⏳ Budget — the run limit and the trades

**What this is:** the **resource layer** of the achievement system — the hard facts that make every choice a **trade**: ~100 years, one run, a mix of **need and want**, a **place you want to be**, and
the **art of enjoying the board you're on.** Pairs with [play § credits](../framework/play.md#-credits--spend-bar) and [pleasure.md](../framework/pleasure.md). **Metaphor only.**

---

## ⏳ Hard limit — ~100y, one run

The most abstracted, obvious, and ignored fact: **life has a hard limit** — eventually, at something like **100 years old**, the run ends.

- **No respawn**, no extra save, no NG+. One instance ([worlds § two instances](../media/worlds.md#%EF%B8%8F-two-instances-not-one-flow)).
- Time is the **ultimate scarce resource** — every entry on the list draws from the same budget.
- The **credits bar** ([play § credits](../framework/play.md#-credits--spend-bar)) is real and finite.

So the whole question is **allocation**: which 80%s, which trades, which folder to deepen — *given the clock is ticking.*

---

## ⚖️ Need vs want mix

A healthy run is a **mix of need and want** — not a purity contest:

<!-- begin table -->
| Extreme | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Want only** | Raw pleasure, rising baseline, no anchor — the [cocaine read](../framework/pleasure.md#%EF%B8%8F-raw-ceiling-vs-sustainable) |
| **Need only** | Safe, empty, maximal lifespan — "living in a bed" as a life plan |
| **Mixed** | Needs keep the board green; wants provide the spikes |
<!-- end table -->

> Diet for health, **time allocation for happiness** ([pleasure § personal fit](../framework/pleasure.md#-personal-fit--not-everyone-suits-everything)).

## 🗺️ The place you want to be

Not every run needs a single **platinum checkbox**. The softer, often better target: **pursue being in the place you want to be.**

<!-- begin table -->
| Approach | Character |
| --- | --- |
| **One dream** | Sharp, brittle, [vacuum after](./platinum.md#%EF%B8%8F-post-achievement-vacuum--what-now) |
| **A place** | Direction, not destination — a state you aim at and can feel when near |
<!-- end table -->

It's about **doing a mix of what we need and what we want** while steering toward the place — [forward vs lateral moves](../framework/possibilities.md#%EF%B8%8F-forward-vs-backward-vs-lateral) on the map, not
one boss fight.

---

## 🔄 Trades must happen

**Lots of trades must happen.** Pursuing one place costs the others — and that's *correct*, not a bug.

<!-- begin table -->
| Spend | Bought |
| --- | --- |
| ~1h/day on an instrument | Years of guitar at 80%+ |
| ~12h/day on a game | Deep fun, no income — a trade, not free |
| A country, a career, a relationship | The place you wanted to be |
<!-- end table -->

**Naming the trade is half the decision.** If you don't know what a choice is spending, you can't tell if it's worth it.

---

## 🌿 Enjoy with what you have

The finishing skill: **learn to enjoy life with what you have** — while still steering toward more.

- Baseline calibration ([pleasure § spike-with-boring-baseline](../framework/pleasure.md#-spike-with-boring-baseline-model)): the boring baseline is *by design* — it's what makes the spikes spike.
- 6/10 satisfaction today beats waiting to be 10/10 someday.
- The Hokage's achievement list is **optionality**, not obligation ([truco § optionality](../lore/naruto/truco-hierarchy.md#-optionality-the-hokages-achievement-list)).
