# ♻️ Tiny self-maintaining environment — the closed loop

**What this is:** a **perpetual-value wheel** — a small, self-sustaining environment built on **closed loops**: reusable water, self-producing food, and (near a volcano) renewable energy. It's the
"one greenhouse that never needs hand-feeding" problem — the wheel that *could* spin forever, but needs the design to close each loop first. Related to [sewage](../sewage/readme.md)
(the pipe system) and [hunger-fund](../hunger-fund/readme.md) (the endowment).

## 📋 Problem statement

<!-- begin table -->
| Axis | Answer | Note |
| ------ | -------- | ------ |
| **Problem** | Build a tiny environment where water and food are **reused indefinitely** and energy is renewable | (not consumed once) — a self-maintaining system instead of a supply chain you keep restocking |
| **Population affected** | One household to a small community (order of magnitude: 1–100 people) | the unit is small, but it's the template for a self-sustaining settlement |
| **Time to implement** | Months to a few years of buildout: closed-loop plumbing, growing systems, energy plant | a designed build, not a one-off patch |
| **Lifespan** | Indefinite **iff** every loop is truly closed — otherwise the wheel decays into a normal supply chain | water purified and returned; food regrown from its own waste; energy generated on site |
<!-- end table -->

## ⚙️ The wheel model

Close each loop so nothing exits and nothing needs refilling:

<!-- begin table -->
| Loop | Input it removes | How it closes |
| ------ | ----------------- | --------------- |
| **Water** | Fresh water refill | Purify and reuse — greywater, condensation, filtration: the same water spins forever |
| **Food** | Grocery refill | Grow food from its own waste — compost → soil → plants → food → compost |
| **Energy** | Grid dependency | Renewable on site; geothermal if near a volcano — enough to even run air conditioning |
<!-- end table -->

**The leverage rule:** the system is a wheel. The moment any loop loses its return path, it becomes a normal one-way supply line and the wheel is broken. Close all three and it self-sustains.

## 💧 Water — reusable infinite

The goal is **infinite reusable water** — the same molecules circulating. Rain capture, greywater separation, filtration and purification, condensation recovery. As long as every drop returns to the
loop, you never refill.

## 🍽️ Food — self-maintaining

Food production that **re-feeds itself**: composting waste back into soil, aquaponics/hydroponics where fish waste fertilizes plants, seasonal crops that don't deplete the medium. The food loop's
output becomes its own input.

## ⚡ Energy — renewable, volcano-adjacent

If the environment sits **close to a volcano**, geothermal is the lever: heat from the ground generates power **and** runs air conditioning without the grid. The heat is the resource — reusable like
the water, not consumed.

## 🛡️ The protected form — layers of resistance

Explore forms of protected place with **as many layers of resistance as possible** — a bunker below the ground running on renewable energy:

- **Layers** — each layer slows entry: hardened door, airlock, blast door, a passage maze, a hidden entrance, onion-style rooms with distinct weaknesses. More layers = more time to breach.
- **Renewable energy** — geothermal/battery bank so the protected place runs off-grid; the deeper you go, the more it must self-sustain.
- **The tradeoff** — every layer of protection is also a layer that must be **self-maintained**: water, food, air, energy, all inside. A fortress that needs restocking isn't a closed loop.
