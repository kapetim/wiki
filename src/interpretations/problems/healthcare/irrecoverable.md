# 🛑 Irrecoverable

**What this is:** health conditions with no known cure.

Conditions that never return to baseline. The best is management — slow the worsening, not a cure.

- **HIV** — not curable; antiretroviral medication after exposure suppresses the virus and prevents progression.
- **Bone and nerve damage** — a lost limb does not regrow, and a broken bone at best stays where it is. The worst is
  nerve compression: constant pain and potential loss of movement, and if it sits in the spine it may herniate.
