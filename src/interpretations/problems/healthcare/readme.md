# 🏥 Healthcare — known conditions by outcome

**What this is:** a triage of **known** conditions by how they resolve — the irrecoverable, the unknown, and the
recoverable. Not a wheel: a ledger of what medicine can and cannot undo, with the best response for each.

## 🗂️ Buckets
