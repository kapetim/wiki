# 🌀 Problems — unsolved wheels

**What this is:** **species-scale** unsolved problems read as **wheels** — systems that keep turning and either **re-feed their own damage** or **won't spin forever on their own**. Not the personal
ladder ([possibilities.md](../framework/possibilities.md)) and not the automation fork ([futures.md](../society/futures.md)) — the **"someone should fix this"** shelf,
one wheel at a time.

## 🔁 The wheel lens

Every unsolved problem is one of two wheels:

<!-- begin table -->
| Wheel | Read | Why | Example |
| ------- | ------ | ----- | --------- |
| **Perpetual-damage** | The wheel **re-feeds its own harm** — every year the same input, the same cost, the same smell | Cleaning the output never ends while the input stays open | Untreated sewage refills the river daily |
| **Perpetual-value** | The wheel **could** spin forever and generate value, but it won't start | no principal, no trust, no one willing to not eat the top | A funded endowment that ends hunger |
<!-- end table -->

**"Solved"** is defined the same way for both: **the wheel runs indefinitely without someone hand-feeding it.** You stop the input, or you protect the principal — and then the system self-sustains.

**Government's real job** on this map is the *management of wheels*: close the damage inputs, protect the value principals, and audit the loops so they don't quietly become someone else's feeding
trough.

## 📋 Problem-statement skeleton

Each file follows the same shape so problems are comparable on three axes:

<!-- begin table -->
| Axis | Question | What a good answer looks like |
| ------ | ---------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Population affected** | Who feels this wheel, in how many people? | Order-of-magnitude count + who specifically |
| **Time to implement** | How long until the fix is built and running? | Buildout + rollout, not wishful one-off |
| **Lifespan** | Once built, does it last without hand-feeding? | Indefinite with maintenance, or decays by design |
<!-- end table -->

A problem is "worth it" when **lifespan × population** dwarfs **time × cost**. Cheap-to-run and long-lived wins; expensive-to-maintain and short-lived loses.

## 🏛️ Government redesign prompt

**"How would I organize government better than the crap they claim to do?"** — the design exercise lives here, applied wheel by wheel:

- **Close the damage inputs** — find where the perpetual-damage wheel is fed and make the *feeder* pay, not the downstream cleaner.
- **Protect the value principals** — a funded wheel must outlive the politicians who started it; lock the top, publish the ledger.
- **Audit the loop** — who profits from the wheel *staying broken*? If a contractor or a bureaucracy earns more from the damage than from the fix, the wheel is being fed on purpose. See
  [control pole](../society/futures.md#-control-pole-who-steers-the) for who holds the veto.

Each problem folder ends with **❓ Open questions** — the investigation still owed, not a promise that the wheel is solved.
