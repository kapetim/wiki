# 🧰 Prepare — tooling and the seed

**What this is:** the reusable half — the indoor setup you buy once and the gear you keep, plus how to start a seed.
The plant-specific maintenance lives in [grow.md](grow.md). No sunlight required. **Not a guarantee.**

## 🧰 Reusable tooling

Bought once, used every cycle. This is the indoor substitute for sun and weather.

<!-- begin table -->
| Tool | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **LED grow light** (full-spectrum board) | The sun replacement — drives the whole cycle; adjust height as the plant grows |
| **Container / pot** | Holds the substrate and roots; fabric pots air-prune and reduce root binding |
| **Inline exhaust fan** | Air exchange — temperature, humidity, and odor |
| **Carbon / odor filter** | Neutralizes smell when the plant gets fragrant |
| **Digital timer** | Automates the light schedule so day/night is exact |
| **Thermo-hygrometer** | Reads temperature and humidity (probe preferred) |
<!-- end table -->

> Airflow alone is not enough once the plant is mature — attach the filter to the exhaust before the smell peaks.

## 💧 Starting the seed

Two common routes — pick one, keep it consistent:

<!-- begin table -->
| Route | How | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paper towel** | Damp towel in a warm dark spot until the taproot shows, then plant | Visible progress; easy to over-handle |
| **Direct to soil** | Plant ~1 cm deep in pre-moistened substrate | No transplant shock; less visible early |
<!-- end table -->

1. **Hydrate** the substrate first — water until a few drops drain, then let it settle.
2. **Plant** the seed ~1 cm deep, cover loosely, and mist lightly.
3. **Warm + dark** until it breaks the surface (a few days), then switch on the light.
4. **Set the light far** at first (roughly 40–50 cm) and bring it closer as leaves form.

## 🪴 Potting

- Fill the container to ~2 cm below the rim; do not compact the substrate.
- Keep the surface gently moist — not soaked — until the first true leaves.
- Label the start date; the timeline in [grow.md](grow.md) counts from here.
