# 🌿 Grow — maintenance without sunlight

**What this is:** the plant-specific half — keeping it alive and productive indoors under a light: the schedule,
watering, feeding, and the stages from seedling to harvest. Setup lives in [prepare.md](prepare.md).

## 📅 Stages

Weeks are approximate and drift with the plant and environment.

<!-- begin table -->
| Stage | Rough weeks | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Seedling** | 1 – 3 | No food — the seed and substrate carry it. Water only when the top 2–3 cm is dry. Light ~40 cm. |
| **Vegetative** | 3 – 5+ | Grow nutrients at ~50% label dose; the plant stretches. Light ~30–35 cm. |
| **Flowering** | 5 – 9 | Switch to bloom nutrients; alternate feed and plain water. Watch odor — filter on. |
| **Finish** | 9 – 11 | Plain water only for the last 10–14 days; harvest when the plant signals ripeness. |
<!-- end table -->

## 🌗 Light schedule

The schedule *is* the season indoors — flip it to tell the plant to stop growing and start flowering.

<!-- begin table -->
| Phase | Hours on / off | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vegetative** | 18 / 6 | Long day — leaf and stem growth |
| **Flowering** | 12 / 12 | Short day — triggers flower and fruit |
<!-- end table -->

A timer keeps this exact; keep the dark period truly dark.

## 💧 Watering & feeding

- **Check before watering** — water when the top layer is dry, not on a fixed clock.
- **Feed, then flush** — alternate *water + nutrients* → *plain water* → *water + nutrients*.
- **Dose** nutrients at ~half the label at first and raise only if the plant asks for it.
- **Flush at the end** — plain water for the last stretch clears residual salts.

## 🌡️ Environment

- Keep temperature and humidity in a steady band; swings stress the plant.
- **Airflow** — an exhaust keeps heat and humidity down and moves fresh CO₂ in.
- **Odor** — attach the carbon filter before the smell peaks, not after.

## ✅ Harvest

Harvest when the plant signals ripeness (color, firmness, or trichomes on the real plant — read the species).
Dry and cure before storing; a rushed dry loses more than any feeding mistake.
