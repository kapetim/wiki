# 👮 Police — the enforcement layer

**What this is:** the generic read on policing — presence as fear, investigation arriving after the fact, and why
enforcement is a **toll** rather than a resolution. Pairs with [fair-game.md](fair-game.md) and
[evidence.md](evidence.md). **Metaphor only, not legal advice.**

- **Tactical absurdity** — the police hold an overwhelming informational and material advantage and don't use it:
  guns plus known criminal locations, tanks, anesthetic weapons, drones with sedative guns. Either incompetence or
  complicity.
- **Rio / favela** — factions' locations are well known; a single protected mission could warn civilians and resolve
  the area. Organized buildings with cameras would make crime very low — with recordings, being caught is
  near-inevitable.
- **Priorities** — lots of drugs collected, few criminals caught; it reads as a pursuit of drugs, not of criminals.
  Anyone can plant cocaine or cannabis, so the focus looks like a dog chasing its own tail.
- **The wheel** — investigation arrives after the fact; the irreversible damage is already done. Patrols as
  presence / fear, a priority queue, reversible vs irreversible, the prison inversion (the attacker gets rent and
  food; the victim keeps the damage), and corruption hiding the culprit. The leverage is to prevent the **input**,
  not clean the **output**.

## ❓ Open questions

- Does enforcement ever reduce the wheel, or only move the damage?
- When is investigation worth it at all versus prevention?
