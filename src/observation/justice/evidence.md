# 🧾 Evidence — proof in the AI age

**What this is:** what "proof" means now that **any recordable thing is just data, and data can be generated**. Video
is a sequence of pictures; audio is a combination of sound waves; if both can be stored on a disk, both can be copied,
modified — or faked from nothing.

Courts once trusted the recording; now they must trust the **provenance** of the bytes. Pairs with
[monitoring.md](../monitoring.md) and [fair-game.md](fair-game.md). **Metaphor only, not legal advice.**

---

## 💾 Data-on-disk principle

If it's possible to **record** something in real life, it becomes a bunch of **data on a disk**. You can also
**generate** the same data on a disk. It's a matter of **how you do it**.

<!-- begin table -->
| Medium | What it is | Implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Video** | A sequence of pictures | Can be edited, copied, or generated frame by frame |
| **Audio** | A combination of sound waves | Can be sampled, spliced, or synthesized |
| **Any record** | A database log | Can be replaced — the "original" is whatever the current copy says |
<!-- end table -->

Recording and generation produce **the same bytes**. The eye and ear can't tell the difference — only the **paper
trail** can.

---

## 🎞️ Forgery cost curve

It was always possible to craft fake proof — or hide the truth — but it used to be **very problematic**:

- **Before:** Photoshop and frame-by-frame video editing — **lots of effort** required to reach perfection. A bad
  edit was catchable.
- **Now:** **AI generates video directly** — a short path, no editors needed. The cost of a convincing fake collapsed.

The barrier to synthetic proof isn't skill anymore; it's **access to a model**.

---

## 🗄️ Log-override metaphor

A recording is just a **database log**. To forge a past, you don't argue with the recording — you **replace the data**:

- Go to a bank and **swap their HDD** with the information you want.
- Clouds add **layers and backups** — but if you override **all of them at the same time**, you have **overridden a
  log**.

There's no sacred original. There's only **how many independent copies** you'd have to rewrite — which is why
**external, uncontrolled copies** are the only real evidence.

---

## 📡 What this does to surveillance

Monitoring § VAR assumed the **perfect recording convicts instantly** — angles, replay, geometry matching, automatic
verdict. The AI age breaks that:

- The "perfect recording" is now **exactly what can be generated**.
- Courts can't lean on pixels — they need **provenance**: where did these bytes come from, and who vouches for them?
- Illegal capture is already unusable ([fair-game.md](fair-game.md)). Synthetic capture pushes
  further: even *legal-looking* bytes aren't proof without a chain.

Watching ≠ proving, and now **capturing ≠ proving** either.

## ⏳ Prosecution timeline — the caught-standard

The practical standard ends up being: **don't make mistakes in front of the police.**

- Anything else is like a **perfect crime** — there may be clues, detectives may have **100% certainty** of the
  culprit, but the person only goes to jail when the judge has **legal evidence**, which loops back to some kind of
  **being caught while doing it**.
- **Crimes have a lifespan to be judged.** Very rare to be accused of something too old. What valid proof appears
  only **N years** after the crime — and why take so long to accuse if the evidence was strong? The expected frame
  is to be **hunted within about a year**; if not caught in that window, you've somewhat **escaped**.
- **Exceptions:** people flagged as criminals who were never found. As long as they **remain with their mask**
  ([masks.md](../masks.md)), it should be fine — like a murderer who swapped countries and never returns. Airports
  have security that would catch departure; the softer routes are **private transport or lower-security facilities**
  (sailing, ship).
- Common failure: the person **goes back to the original country** thinking the police forgot.

## 🕵️ Framing needs an origin story

You can **plant** a situation on someone:

- Put **contraband and cash** in a bag, throw it onto someone, call the police — they may instantly be treated as a
  criminal, **without having done anything**.
- Same at home: plant an incriminating object, move some money around, throw it on anyone. **As long as the police
  believe, that person is truly a criminal** in the file.

But it's **expected to be investigated** — and frames fail on **provenance**:

> How did the object and the money appear? There's **no paper trail**, no **withdrawal from their account**, no
> **contact with a counterparty**.

Nothing structurally prevents a frame — but **having a normal life is the best protection**: a consistent, auditable
origin story that a planted object can't match.

## 🚗 Self-defense and the unrecorded room

Accept car damage over time. Relying on a front camera only helps you **collect evidence to keep complaining to a
judge later** — reasonable, but better to focus on self-defense.

If there's no live stream, anything done in a bedroom is valid: a person may **claim self-defense** for whatever
happened there — who has any proof of the scene? Only if you're live streaming can it be explained. The claim's blind
spot is [self-defense.md](self-defense.md).
