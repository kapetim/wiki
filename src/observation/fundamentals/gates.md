# 🚪 Gates — rooms, flags, and endless stairs

**What this is:** a game-stage read on **which rooms in life actually work for you** — welcomed vs forbidden doors, hidden prerequisites (**flags**), and the trap of **endless stairs** (effort that
feels like forward motion). The wisdom: **spend most days in rooms that work.** Pairs with [returns.md](../../achievements/returns.md) (effort allocation) and [harm.md](./harm.md) (the loop).
**Metaphor only.**

---

## 🏰 Stage model

<!-- begin table -->
| Layer | What it feels like |
| --- | --- |
| **Rooms** | Jobs, relationships, scenes, identities, social circles |
| **Welcomed** | Door opens; low friction; you leave **happier** or **useful** |
| **Forbidden** | Polite no, cold shoulder, endless interview loop — **reason often unknown** |
| **Flag** | Hidden prerequisite — skill, timing, consent, reputation, chemistry, luck |
| **Decoration reskin** | "New" room that is the **same script** — different wallpaper |
<!-- end table -->

**Unknown access rules:** some people walk into certain rooms with ease while you hit a soft wall. That's not always injustice — often you lack a **flag** you can't see yet, or the room was never
yours. Hammering a forbidden door usually costs more than it pays.

---

## 🎮 Mario 64 — Bowser's endless stairs

Inside Bowser's castle, the long staircase behaves like an **endless stair** until a **progress flag** is satisfied (enough Power Stars). Mario runs; the camera
moves — but you don't reach the top while the flag is **off**. After the flag flips, the same staircase is **trivial**. The geometry didn't change — **you** did.

<!-- begin table -->
| Layer | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Flag off** | Climbing is a **loop** — effort without arrival |
| **Flag on** | Same stairs **work** after the main-quest milestone |
| **Trick / hack** | Only counts if it **actually works** on your build |
<!-- end table -->

Many "impossible" rooms in life are that simple once the right prerequisite exists.

| Forbidden room | Soft or hard no; reason may be opaque |

| Welcomed queue | Known-good rooms while a gate stays closed |
<!-- end table -->

## 🧭 Default routing

1. **Ignore forbidden** unless strictly necessary now.
2. If you must engage: check for a real trick; if none, **main quest** (build flags) or **welcomed queue**.
3. **Infinite climb** only when nothing better to do — boredom burn, not heroism.

**Where does the effort best go?** — the same principle drives [returns.md § allocation](../../achievements/returns.md#-generalist-vs-specialist-allocation): put hours where they pay, not where they only
feel heroic.

## 🎒 Dora pass — new places (bounded)

*Dora the Explorer* script: **map → new place → one challenge → back home.** Useful when you want novelty and can pay the anxiety tax.

- **Map** — name one truly new room, not a reskin.
- **Go** — short visit; one concrete action.
- **Face unknown** — new place = unknown situations = anxiety = an informal life test.
- **Home** — debrief; note what worked.

**Reskin trap:** after enough exploration you notice many "new" rooms are the same loop — different decorations, same script. That's not failure; your search radius needs **depth, not width**.

**Not everyone is an explorer:** some builds run best on welcomed repeats and main quest. Forcing Dora mode out of FOMO rarely unlocks flags.
