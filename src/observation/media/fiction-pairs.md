## 📖 How to use

1. Read the **sociology** (or psychology) section once for the handle.
2. Rewatch the **story** with one question from that section.
3. Log new confusion in the franchise `questions.md` — not in sociology unless the theory itself breaks.

Do **not** paste full theory essays into franchise folders — one-line **Philosophy read:** on the franchise file is enough.
