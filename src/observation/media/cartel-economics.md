## 🔎 Issue read

- Person joins cartel with **good management ideas** — Pablo Escobar archetype
- Most tactics were **possible but dangerous**; safer years ago with fewer security layers
- **Today:** mostly groups with government relations; hard to ship without notice
- Still operates because **buyers stay quiet** and sellers stay calm — last mile is civilians near police
- **Hero cop pursuit** jeopardizes the plan — one officer on a mission breaks equilibrium

## 🏛️ Institutions colliding

Many plots are **institutions fighting** — DEA, hospitals, cartel hierarchy, legal law — not lone genius. Walter's pride and Jimmy's slide are **local** versions of the same macro.
