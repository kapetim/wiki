## 🔧 Custom made

What about transplants or augmentation — custom-made shit?

- A transplant is possible but rare; credibility is **body-bound** — who believes you? This ties to the anti-theft read in [balance.md](../../framework/balance.md) (embodied identity as the real
  anti-theft).
- **Physical limit** — erectile maintenance requires sustained blood flow. Anything too large is hard to maintain erect without risking cardiovascular strain — a heart attack. So there is a soft
  ceiling well below any unbounded claim.
- Even if you could add size, the compensation rule still applies — attention gained must be matched by wealth held.
