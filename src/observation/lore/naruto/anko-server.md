# 🌸 Anko — the most desired woman

**What this is:** the human capstone of the village reads — a woman's position between god and devil, the server she controls, and the backyard of defined borders. Concludes the sequence started in
[power-guns.md](./power-guns.md), [orchestrator.md](./orchestrator.md), and [infinite-loop.md](./infinite-loop.md). **Metaphor only.**

## 🎭 God vs devil

Anko is in a tricky situation. She was the favorite student of Hiruzen, who happened to be bitten by the devil snake while young — it clearly shows some kind of rapist
behavior. The odd thing is that she started looking for some protection, like going to church to not be sent to hell and keep being bitten by snakes out of nowhere.

The problem is that she ended up close to another rapist — still, someone better. At least he seems to have some standards and warns before such things: like declaring love to someone and asking to
have
sex. Better than being raped out of nowhere with no warning. This somewhat makes Anko the most desired woman: god and devil are fighting for her respect, still none seems to have it.

- **God** — the current leader of the village, trying to hide his acts of rape.
- **Devil** — pretty much the same, but a bit younger; was sent to some random place out of the village due to being accused of biting a woman's neck with a snake without her consent.

If you look close, they both are the same — one just seems more delicate and hides better; the other is more upfront and simply does what he wants.

## 🖥️ The server

Perhaps she actually wanted to have sex, but the goal is not necessarily to be raped, nor to keep receiving infinite requests in her server about such things. The perfect
world seems to be in the middle: set up a server and put a **rate limiter**, somewhat handling requests as possible. Still, the server must run somewhere:

- **Clouds** — if the leader of the village is the owner of the cloud server, then you got tricked: believing you host your server in a place you were trying to avoid.
- **No server** — simply not having one, but then she loses purpose in life.
- **Private server** — her own computer: the most secure way, but it prevents requests from everywhere; it requires internet connection, which can be sabotaged by simply not showing requests they
  don't want to.

It is quite odd — Anko holds an odd place, similar to Shisui: too afraid of the devil but not in love with the Hokage, and the village is afraid of her due to her curse mark. So either she fucks with
the devil or with god.

## ⚔️ The 4th option

There may be a 4th dude — Kabuto during the war. Consider Shisui to be in the middle as some good option, but too gay; then you have Kabuto with some life standards and not gay. That's complicated. A
woman's life
is generally built around doing your best to be wanted but somewhat in control of what happens. She sits in the middle of hell and heaven; still, Shisui is too gay and Kabuto is too aggressive.

## 🏡 The backyard

It is like a diamond: even if you sit in the middle, it gets boring. Better to look into it as a backyard — you have defined borders of your home for a reason, some agreed space in which no one
enters. Still, quite boring to put a chair right in the middle, and somewhat bad to keep running only.

It is like trying to define as many combinations as possible and repeat the best ones: walk for a while, run for a while, sit for a while, sleep for a while, eat for a while, and poop for a while.
Anything else seems to be too much — a detail between these.

## 🎬 Conclusion — the organization on top of criminality

The anime somewhat shows how the world is organized: an organization on top of criminality in the world.

Shisui's arc ends here: he had a back problem and started saying shit about the Hokage. Then he stole his eye and believed Itachi killed him — still, not capable of
using his power. Kept pursuing Anko's respect up to finding his death at the village. Interesting enough, he eventually got revived during the war.

If life is a game, everything is possible: simply collect the metadata of a Hokage and spawn as many instances as you need — keeping it to just one with some rules makes people believe more into it.
