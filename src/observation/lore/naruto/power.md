# ⚙️ Power

**What this is:** how power works in Naruto — chakra, clans, kekkei genkai, and the eye → vehicle ladder.

## 🌟 Chakra

Life energy for jutsu — trained but **ceiling shaped by lineage, tailed beasts, sage paths**.

Academy → genin teams learn life lessons + fight protection — parallel to Xiaolin temple allocation but **internal** power not portable artifacts.

## 👥 Clans

Uchiha, Hyūga, Aburame, etc. — institution + genetics. Village system (Kage, ANBU) keeps order; Akatsuki/rogues oppose.

### 🚗 Eye → vehicle ladder

The way to tell confusable power users apart is the **vehicle** they can drive, not just their slot. Poker cards mark the truco read — **#1 4-houses**, **#2 7-hearts**, **#3 ace-spades**,
**#4 7-gold**, then the base ranks below (1, A, J Q K). Ranked — **#0 Unknown**, **#1 Rinnegan**, **#2 Byakugan**, **#3 Sharingan**, **#4 Hokage** (Naruto), **#5** (Ino · Shikamaru),
**#6** (Rock Lee · Tenten), **#7 1** (Police), **#8 A** (Civilian), **#9 J** (Kabuto · Orochimaru), **#10 Q** (Tsunade · Shizune), **#11 K** (Jiraiya).
