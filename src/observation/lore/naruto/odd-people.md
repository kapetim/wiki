# 👥 Odd people & the obedience pyramid

**What this is:** notes on the odd people of the village — the ones who didn't follow the reproduction or obedience game — plus the pyramid of convincing power they sit under and the Shisui war it
started from. Pairs with [anko-server.md](./anko-server.md), [orchestrator.md](./orchestrator.md), [truco-hierarchy.md](./truco-hierarchy.md), and [infinite-loop.md](./infinite-loop.md).
**Metaphor only.**

## 🎲 The power competition

The anime is some competition of power — everyone trying to say they would win but never truly winning shit. Translate it to convincing power: the strongest person simply says half a word and everyone
obeys without questioning.

## 🔺 The obedience pyramid

From there you have pyramid layers of obedience: Shisui obedient to Orochimaru and Kabuto, Itachi obedient to Shisui, Sasuke obedient to Itachi, Sarada obedient to Sasuke. It's quite hard to go
through such shit.

**The Sims metaphor** — it's like saying one sim from the Sims will command the person playing the game. They can simply restart the game and force some shit. Cross-refs: the
[war map](./hell-heaven-bridge.md) (sound village → orochimaru → kabuto → shisui) and [infinite-loop.md](./infinite-loop.md) (the same boss with upgrades).

## 🔮 The possibilities

The anime plays around possibilities — each generation shows them during war and is eventually limited by the kekkei genkai.

- **An Uchiha having a baby with a Byakugan user is not feasible** — it would make a person with Rinnegan in the village: someone impossible to control, somewhat putting the Hokage in check.
- **They forbid male Uchiha in the village** — afraid of what can happen.
- If your goal is to command everyone, someone with Rinnegan will put the Hokage under some trouble.
- They prefer to believe Pain has died and that he commands everyone else.

**The 7-gold Hokage** — this makes the Hokage the 7-gold shit: he prefers to live as the best and forget about the other 3 cards. There may actually be more than 3 — you can see how populated the
orphanage is. See [truco-hierarchy.md](./truco-hierarchy.md) for the trump definitions.

**Kabuto & the orphanage** — they probably trust Kabuto after he stopped the war by himself. The orphanage also works as a helper tool for Sarada: whenever she's bored with the village, she can join
the
orphanage to remember why she must keep training. If Kabuto's army is stronger than her, what about Orochimaru's army? The Hokage at least provides her with some security and entertainment.

## ⚔️ The Shisui war origin

The anime probably started with some war around Shisui — saying not even the Hokage can convince him — somewhat translated into a bait to make the Hokage believe he was dead, but his eye continued
working masked as one of Itachi's crows.

He probably passed with it because the Hokage is too old and can't see it properly. He also doesn't want to become a reviewer of crows to identify which one is Shisui. He kept believing his stolen eye
was working, but still no one obeyed him.

Probably some narrative to show what he used to do with other people: **obey my command or I steal your eye**. Then Shisui somewhat won against him.

**The teleportation question** — what prevented him from simply teleporting after throwing a genjutsu? The teleportation guy would remain stopped, waiting to be attacked by everyone else.

**The stupid army** — they have a completely stupid and delusional leader, creating an army of stupid people. This event somewhat explains they were fooled their
entire life by investing their time into silly strategies that don't harm. Some people can only obey through pain after receiving some attack.

**The herbivore show** — the herbivore group creates lots of dance moves and offensive words; then a blind and deaf person wins without doing shit. Cross-ref the herbivore-vs-carnivore read in
[infinite-loop.md](./infinite-loop.md).

## 🔚 Close

The village is a competition of convincing power with a pyramid of obedience underneath — most people never truly win, some are odd enough to sit outside the game, and the whole thing keeps spinning
as an infinite loop.
