# 💭 Sentiment — the felt layer

**What this is:** sentiment as the felt layer — how things land emotionally.

**Emotions and attachment** as you actually experience them — not harm accounting (../fundamentals/) and not spawn metaphor (born). Bridges **memory** (seasons) and **social skin**
([masks](./masks.md)).

## 🗺️ Core map

<!-- begin table -->
| Family | Feels like | Body hook | Common trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fear / anxiety** | Narrow future, escape urge | Chest, gut, restless legs | Uncertainty, social threat, health scare |
| **Anger** | Heat, push, “unfair” | Jaw, fists, loud voice | Boundary crossed, blocked goal |
| **Sadness / grief** | Heavy, slow, empty | Tears, fatigue | Loss, failure, loneliness |
| **Shame** | Want to hide, small | Face hot, eyes down | Exposure, moral stain, rejection |
| **Guilt** | Owes repair | Stomach knot | Harm done (real or imagined) |
| **Disgust** | Recoil, contaminate | Nausea, grimace | Rot, betrayal, “wrong” sex/food |
| **Joy / relief** | Open, light | Smile, exhale | Goal met, danger passed |
| **Love / warmth** | Pull toward, safe | Soft chest, calm breath | Bond, beauty, belonging |
| **Nostalgia** | Sweet + ache | Lump throat | Past place, dead era, younger self |
<!-- end table -->

**Duration axis** (pairs with [judgement § duration](./judgement.md)): flash anger vs years of grief — same label, different repair time.

## 🧷 Attachment (rough)

Not a diagnosis — **pattern handles** for how you reach for others after stress.

<!-- begin table -->
| Pattern | Under stress you… | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Secure** | Ask, repair, tolerate distance | None magic — still hurt |
| **Anxious** | Chase reassurance, read silence as doom | Burn relationships |
| **Avoidant** | Withdraw, minimize need | Partner feels shut out |
| **Disorganized** | Approach + flee same person | Chaos after trauma |
<!-- end table -->

**Season files** often show pattern shifts (isolation years vs reunion) — seasons.

## 🎭 Shame vs guilt (practical)

<!-- begin table -->
| | **Guilt** | **Shame** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Focus** | “I did bad” | “I am bad” |
| **Repair** | Apologize, fix, pay | Hide, attack self, perform purity |
| **Mask link** | Sometimes healthy conscience | Often feeds [masks § self-protection](./masks.md#%EF%B8%8F-self-protection-why-the-mask-exists) |
<!-- end table -->

**Street vs court:** law-street-trust — shame is social **before** evidence exists.

## 🎁 Sentiment wrapping trades

People rarely say “I am buying attention for X.” They say love, calling, patriotism, art — [masks](./masks.md). **Sentiment is the UI** on
[behavior § incentives](media/behavior.md#-incentives-and-scripts).

<!-- begin table -->
| Wrapped as | Often paying for |
| --- | --- |
| “True love” | Security, status, sex, care labor |
| “Loyalty” | Silence about harm |
| “Passion” | Overwork tolerance |
| “Family honor” | Control of members |
<!-- end table -->

**Not cynical-only:** real warmth exists — but **mixed motives** are default, not scandal.
