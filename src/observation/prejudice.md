# 🔎 Prejudice — labelizing as a hypothesis

**What this is:** **prejudice** as an **initial assumption** — a label used to organise data and aim a query, to be proved (or dropped) later. Not a verdict. Pairs with
[judgement.md](judgement.md) (ranking), [masks.md](masks.md) (skins), and [society/colors.md](society/colors.md) (groups).

---

## 🧪 Prejudice = a hypothesis

A prejudice is a **first label from partial signal** — a guess, a stepping stone toward the target, **to be proved later**. Every guess engine works this way: it labels from an incomplete input and
refines from there.

## 🎯 Guess engines — input → output

<!-- begin table -->
| Engine | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shazam** | a short audio clip (sound waves) | the song — title · artist |
| **Akinator** | tailored yes/no answers | the person |
| **Pokédex** | a picture | the pokémon (+ its type) |
| **AI** | an input string | the output string |
<!-- end table -->

Each maps an **incomplete input** to a **label**, then refines — that is the whole move.

## 🗂️ Labelizing organizes data

An **alphabet** and boxes (`A/`, `B/`, …) beat one giant pile: **divide and conquer** makes queries cheap and reaches the target quicker. Without Shazam it is hard to discover which music you just
heard; without a Pokédex you can never know the type of the other pokémon. Labels **prepare the next step**.

## 💥 Why a wrong label hurts

A label is not neutral — a **wrong value causes unfairness**. A Pokédex that says **Charmander is a water type** invites you to give it a house in the lake — and it **dies**. The purpose of the label is
to prevent exactly that.

## ⚔️ Duels vs important things

Comparisons and **duels are generally quick and inside a controllable environment** — a safe place to test a label and settle it. Mess with **important** things instead and you **invite chaos** and
lose the **principles of fairness**.

## 📏 Bare-minimum standards

It is about **recognising the bare minimum standard for each person or pokémon**. Without it we go back to the **dinosaur era — no rules, no standards → war and survival**. The work here is a
**mission to prevent that**: label honestly so the baseline is honoured.

Like picking a **cat out of the sewage and putting it back in the streets** — it was not made to swim, and it **drowns**. A label that ignores the baseline kills.

## ⚖️ Comparison rules (community)

- **Compare traits, not people.**
- Avoid direct comparisons the community hates — e.g. **people compared to animals** — even when the logic holds; pick an accepted analogue (actor, genius). Strange, but real.
- **Prejudice is allowed as an assumption to prove**, never as a posted fact.
- The hard line is enforced: slurs are forbidden by the content validator (`scripts/checks/content.js` FORBID).
