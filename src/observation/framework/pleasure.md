# 🌶️ Pleasure — baseline vs spike

**What this is:** how enjoyment depends on **contrast to your baseline**, not on absolute stimulus. The same thing that delights from a low baseline barely registers from a high one — so the skill is
tuning **frequency**, not chasing a constant peak. Pairs with [balance.md](./balance.md) (the tuner) and [play.md](./play.md) (credits / life dream). **Metaphor only.**

---

## ⬆️ Raw ceiling vs sustainable

From a raw perspective, **cocaine** provides the brain with the most entertaining sensation. But doing it every time makes it **less pleasurable**, and you also **can't enjoy the normal things** the
way you did — the baseline has been raised.

---

## 🥩 Meat metaphor — baseline calibration

- If you're used to eating cheap meat, a **wagyu is sensational**.
- If you eat wagyu **every day**, you'll have trouble **impressing yourself**.
- Anything different from your standard meal ends up feeling **worse than you're used to**.

---

## 📉 Spike-with-boring-baseline model

It's generally better to surround yourself with **healthy things that are depressive** and **occasionally** provide yourself with something enthusiastic:

> Eat shit every day, once per month eat a wagyu — that moment becomes **way more pleasurable**.

---

## 📊 Configuration — frequency

The configuration part is: **how frequently should each thing be?**

- Diet generally is best planned as **bad-taste healthy food** with **occasional good-taste junk**.
- The **same diet every day forever** is too boring and causes **depression**.

## 🗺️ Map the game — Pokedex, but the collection is silly

It's worth **mapping existing things in the life game** like a Pokedex — but the **collection itself is silly**. The very best use is as a **tool for crafting the perfect routine**:

- If you know all drugs — the right amount, how long to recover — **there's no blocker** for such shit.
- It's like being **adult and responsible**.

## 💊 Responsible substance use

Be aware that cocaine is **good but has a harsh withdrawal**. Allocate it like a **prize for special occasions**, and **learn to continue life afterwards**.

- There's a risk of going into a **deep hole and not being able to get back** — like dropping from a hill on a skateboard: despite being good, there's the risk the
  **next attempt results in an accident**.
- **The better prepared you are, the less likely.** But things only attract **if risky**.
- **Balance** — again.

---

## 🧭 Personal fit — not everyone suits everything

In general, each person has their **own choices of what they prefer**; not everyone is suitable for everything:

- A **disabled person** may be happier with a **very powerful computer**.
- A **healthy person** may be happier with a **good wardrobe of clothes**.

Understanding **who you are**, **which things you like**, and **what makes you happy** helps define your **routine**. It's like:

> **Diet for health, time allocation for happiness.**
