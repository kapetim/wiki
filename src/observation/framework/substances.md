## 🚙 Baseline — the standard car

A normal person is pretty much a **standard car** — no customization, nothing fancy, great at transportation.

- After some time driving, **things get boring** — people start looking for alternatives.
- The car works; the *driver* gets restless. That restlessness is the seed of every substance in this file.

## 🛣️ Why buy a car at all

- It's quite possible to **drive with lots of problems**; it's quite hard to find a car with **no scratch**.
- A no-scratch car only exists **parked at home**. Then why buy a car and not some decorative shit? **Life is meant to be lived.**
- The problem: cars **break**, and not everything can be fixed (spine problems). It's a **trade** — live your life, risk your health, and accept that some problems along the path are **irrecoverable**
  .
- Being only at home provides the **most security** — but then you get boredom and "**why be alive**". And even at home you can make a mistake: **fall by yourself while cleaning the floor**.
- The body that lives longest is the one in a **hospital bed** — no disease contact, no bone problems. But then again: **why be alive?**

## 🎮 Drugs as upgrades + sweet spot

- Drugs make life **more enjoyable than standard** — like a computer with a **custom framework**, a video game with a **mod**, a **better screen display**.
- Most of these upgrades are **replaceable and fixable** — the body recovers a lot from simple damage. But some things don't fix: **HIV, bones, and some mental shit**.

**Be reasonable.** Despite liking to smoke, it's about the sweet spot:

- **3 cigarettes/day is far less harmful than an entire pack.** Best is none — but so is a hospital bed.
- Find your **life goal** and stick to it. Discover **what makes you happiest** and repeat it as many times as possible.
- It's inevitable to get bored and look for something else — that's the loop.
- Each person defines their own "live life" and joy. One framing: **substances as additional stimulation** — an odd fuel, aware of the problems. There is a sweet spot.
- Perhaps living up to **60 with lots of events** is better than **90 years cooking and watching TV** — like cigarettes reducing your life but giving happier moments many times. Without it, you'd
  simply prefer to die of boredom.

## 📅 The routine — lowest-damage build

- Stick to the **lowest-damage routine**, and add **prizes for special occasions**.
- Allow **cocaine when accomplishing a task**: define a milestone of a code project, once released → **go celebrate**.
- Be aware it takes **X days to recover — and so be it**. Respect the clock.

## ⚡ Speed lane (stimulants)

Drugs that push you to **move** — like being anxious, and the relief being to put some effort.

- **Coffee** → get tasks done. Standard cheap gas.
- **Cocaine** → **nitro**. The joker card, works for anything. Emergency mode — *Wolf of Wall Street* spinach scene: you become a superhero without fear and actually get some odd things done.
  - **Painkiller blind spot:** cocaine itself doesn't harm the body, but it's a **natural painkiller** — you can run without a leg and only notice when the effect fades. The risk is the blind spot,
    not the chemistry.
  - **Emergency only:** hard to find a stronger stimulant. Adding it to the routine is bad — when bored under cocaine, you can't find something stronger.
- **Crack** → very strong cocaine.
- **Methamphetamine** → artificial cocaine — similar effects, open question on whether it skips the addiction.
- **Amphetamines** → pharmaceutical coffee — task focus without the nitro kick.

**Usage geometry:**

- Using a stimulant while **doing nothing** is like putting **nitro with the handbrake on** — damages the car and wastes resources.
- The very best is to **pick a straight line and have fun**. Doing it during curves is risky and dangerous.

## 💞 Middle lane — MDMA / ecstasy

Between coffee and cocaine, but a bit different.

- **Custom-made gas** → a **love machine** (*Up in Smoke*). Raises most affection points of the brain; tends to make people **interact more**, attentive to others' sentiments, keep flirting.
- **Partner-finding tool:** great when going to a group of friends and checking who is most likely to succeed with whom.
- **The putaria state:** everyone has sex with everyone — like hidden rules making a perfect fit of everyone. Somewhat **masks reality**.
- But if you pay attention, you **learned what the other person likes** — once sober you can simply redo it knowingly. Helps to comprehend the **mental rules of each person**.
- **Great for parties, dangerous for STDs.** Complicated for **marriage and loyalty**.
- **Almost as powerful as cocaine but not so addictive** — a hack.
- **Expensive, and crosses borders not so easy to undo:** 30 minutes of happiness for 30 days running from the angry spouse (like sex with a married person).

## 🧭 Accuracy lane (slowing down)

Drugs that build **patience** to pick the best choice — good when about to join a curve or maneuver the car toward parking.

- **Cannabis + LSD** — the **creative family**. Waterfall of ideas, wonderland, enhanced world.
- **Tobacco** — the soft brake; slows the water flow.

**Water-flow trio** (three taps on the same stream):

- **Coffee** keeps you running.
- **Cannabis** = a **waterfall of ideas** — with note-taking you keep the best options for the moment, but then you get too tired.
- **Tobacco** slows the water flow down.
- Either way you're exploring the **max of what the car can handle** — like racing without realizing the motor is overheating.

**Tobacco detail:**

- Great **prize after sex or after some work done**; kind of horrible **before** work.
- Works like a **zoom with patience**, but not much energy — good for reviewing previous actions or calming down before the next choice. Doing it during the task is less optimal.
- The rhythm: **drink a coffee, then smoke some shit to calm down** — still better after the task is done.
- Acts as an **addiction stick** for boring tasks: a guaranteed cheap prize that lets you skip reasoning about *why* you're doing the task — at some health cost.

## 🥴 Stupid mode — alcohol (+ poppers)

- Alcohol tends to be a **stupid mode on** — like **closing your eyes while driving**.
- Helps to avoid thinking about choices and simply **pick any path** — sometimes causing accidents from not being attentive.
- Poppers sit nearby: a quick headrush, same dizzy family.

## 🧯 Emergency brakes (graded)

- **Soft brake = tobacco** — gentle settling, good for calming down and reviewing.
- **Handbrake = ketamine** — emergency stopping when the heartbeat runs too high; very calming.
- **Adjacent rows:** benzos (liquid alcohol — anti-anxiety without the intoxication) · nitrous (brief warmth, short window).

## 🧠 Awareness & consequences

- **Know the consequence for each, and be smart about the usage.**
- **Any substance used with frequency builds up dependency.** Using it without the car in a race is not the same thing.
- Sometimes you **break the car** — racing should be stopped until fixed. **Nitrous doesn't fix a broken wheel or some tricky parts.**
- **Overheating:** pushing the max the car can handle (the water-flow trio note).
- **Cocaine ceiling:** when bored under cocaine you can't find anything stronger — you have crack. Then it's quite tricky to manage these shit.

## 🧱 Curiosities — who benefits

- A **creative and stable plan** is part of the **engineer's job** → cannabis / LSD serve the *planning* half: visualize many different forms of how to build it. No heavy lifting — just drawing shit,
  looking forward to a good representation of the project.
- An **efficient soldier** is part of the **bricklayer's job** → coffee / cocaine serve the *execution* half: ignore pain, do the hard repetitive work, feel like a superhero and carry more weight.
- **Both are doable without any drug at all** — but that's life. People can also live by only drinking water and lying in a hospital bed.
- **Cocaine on an engineer** may introduce problems that could have been better planned — daydreaming, skipping things not very likely to happen, too speedy for patience.
- **Cannabis on a bricklayer** → they feel their pain and complain too much.
- **Paranoid planner:** nothing like a paranoid person to build the closest-to-perfect plan — their mind is already like a jar full of holes, used to handling such shit.
- **Cocaine enhances sensations** — doing something painful is not the best combination. The bricklayer's back problem starts getting more noticeable during the construction.
- **Division of labor:** the engineer already thought hard about the rules and wants an **obedient person who is fast**. There's not much creativity required to build a toilet — the faster
  you do it while following the rules, the better. A brilliant idea as a bricklayer doesn't help much; as an engineer it's quite good — you're the one responsible for it.
