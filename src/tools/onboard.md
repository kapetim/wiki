# 🏠 Onboard

**What this is:** the minimal kit to buy when arriving at a new home — grab the cheapest thing that works, use it hard, and bin it on the next move. Never rely on what the stay already provides, and never pay for durability.

## 🧭 Principle

- Buy early and cheap — about six months of service is enough.
- Assume the place has nothing; bring or buy your own.
- Use it hard, bin it on the move, and rebuy at the next stay.
