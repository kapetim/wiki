# 🗳️ Voting

How to vote in Brazil — registration, polling place, and absence justification.

## 🧭 Steps

1. **e-Titulo** — open the app, confirm registration and polling place.
2. **Vote in person** — 8:00–17:00 on election day.
3. **If not in person** — open e-Titulo and file **justificativa de ausência**; it's straightforward and covers you.

## 🗓️ Cadence

- **General** — every 4 years when year % 4 == 2 (2026, 2030, …). 2nd turn only if no majority in the 1st.
- **Municipal** — every 4 years when year % 4 == 0 (2028, 2032, …), offset 2y from general.
- Official dates may shift; confirm when announced.
