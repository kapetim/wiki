# 🧾 IRPF

The annual income tax declaration procedure.

## 🧭 Steps

1. **Watch for opening** — declaration opens in late March; transmit before the deadline (usually end of May).
2. **Collect informes** — every bank, broker, and wallet with balance on 31 December; employer income statement.
3. **Open** PGD or Meu Imposto de Renda; import pre-filled when available.
4. **Income** — enter employer and taxable bank/broker lines from the informes.
5. **Assets** — list accounts and investments at 31/12 balances.
6. **Deductions** — only with proof; compare simplificado vs completo; pick the lower tax.
7. **Review** — zero program warnings; totals match the informes.
8. **Transmit** before the deadline.

## 📤 Upload

Save the transmitted declaração and its recibo as PDFs under `government/brazil/cpf/irpf/<year>/`, then add the pair to the IRPF table in `government/brazil/cpf/finance/readme.md`.
