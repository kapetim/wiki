# 🩺 Service providers

Health and grooming providers — who to book, and the shared routine.

## 🧭 Routine

1. Search nearby providers (reviews, distance, insurance if any).
2. Book the appointment (+ exam if bundled).
3. Attend; note next recommended interval.

## 🗂️ Providers
