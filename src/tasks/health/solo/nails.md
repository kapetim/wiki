# 🦶 Nails

Toenail and fingernail care — done by yourself.

## 🧭 Steps

1. **Prep** — feet clean and dry; easier clip, less slip.
2. **Toes** — clip straight across; do not round corners too short (ingrown prevention).
3. **File** — smooth any sharp edge that could catch socks.
4. **Hand nails** — clip straight, file rough edges; keep slightly above the quick.
5. **Cleanup** — collect clippings; wipe and dry clipper.

## 📁 Notes

- **Ingrown prevention:** straight cut, not curved corners; don't cut into the nail bed.
- **Foot health:** if redness, swelling, or pain — skip aggressive trim; treat before next pass.
- **Socks/shoes:** sharp edges → holes and pressure points; file until smooth.
- Use the **dedicated foot clipper** if separate from the hand clipper; sanitize after use.
