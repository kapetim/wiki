# 🃏 UNO

**The shedding game.** Empty your hand first — the lesson is card counting and timing.

## 🧠 Meaning

The player who burns their hand fastest wins; hold the control cards for the right moment.

## 🗝️ Beat it with ease

- **Count the colors.** Track roughly how many cards each player plays per color; lead into the color they lack.
- **Hold skips and reverses.** They end the turn of the player about to win — don't waste them early.
- **Save the wild +4 for the kill.** When an opponent has one card left, the +4 is a win button — never spend it on a boring turn.
- **Keep a matching color.** Against a +2, always hold at least one card in a color you can play so you don't draw.
- **Go out on +cards.** A +2 or +4 as your final card stops the next player — the perfect finisher.
- **Draw the deck, not your opponent.** When stuck, match the pile color you already hold the most of.
