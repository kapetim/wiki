# 🗼 Jenga

**The stacking game.** Pull blocks and keep the tower standing — the lesson is steady hands and nerve.

## 🧠 Meaning

The tower falls for the player who rushes. Slow wins.

## 🗝️ Beat it with ease

- **Pull from the middle rows.** Mid-tower bricks are loosest and least risky to free.
- **Never the top finished row.** Completed rows settle — disturb them last.
- **Steady two-finger pull.** Flick it out with a fast clean motion; hesitation wobbles the whole stack.
- **Watch the loose brick.** If a brick visibly sticks out, it comes out easily — but check it's not load-bearing first.
- **Don't let the tower lean.** Push back gently toward the side you haven't weakened; a straight tower survives longer.
- **Patience is the weapon.** Let the opponent play faster; their mistakes are your win.
