# ⬛ Checkers

**The jumping game.** Empty the board of enemy pieces — the lesson is trades and positioning.

## 🧠 Meaning

A capture is a trade; the player who controls the trade count wins.

## 🗝️ Beat it with ease

- **Keep the back row intact.** Moving a back-row piece early exposes your rear — leave it until forced.
- **Trade when ahead.** Up one piece, every even exchange closes the game; down one piece, refuse all trades.
- **Force captures with a sacrifice.** Leave a piece as bait, then take two on the return jump.
- **Get to the kings row.** The first player to make kings has a runaway advantage — push the side of the board your opponent has abandoned.
- **Control the center.** Side pieces are boxed in; central pieces jump in four directions.
- **Watch the double jump.** Before any capture, count your opponent's landing square — a land in the middle often buys a follow-up jump.
