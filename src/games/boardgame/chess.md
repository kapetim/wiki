# ♟️ Chess

**The strategy game.** Control the center and convert — the lesson is pattern memory and calculation.

## 🧠 Meaning

Every move is a question about the board; the grind is seeing one move deeper each time.

## 🗝️ Beat it with ease

- **Control the center.** Occupy or pressure e4/d4/e5/d5; a pawn in the middle outposts your pieces.
- **Develop before attacking.** Knights out, bishops out, castle early. Pieces on their first rank defend nothing.
- **Every move has a purpose.** Don't push pawns that don't fight for space or open lines.
- **Don't grab free pawns blindly.** Queens and rooks in the open get hunted — count attackers and defenders first.
- **Trade when ahead, simplify when winning.** Fewer pieces makes a material lead decisive.
- **Endgame shortcut: opposition and the rook.** In king-pawn endgames, shoulder the enemy king; rook endgames want the rook behind the passed pawn.
- **Tempo is a resource.** A check that forces a move gives you two moves in a row — use it to win the race.
