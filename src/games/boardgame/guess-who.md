# 🕵️ Guess Who?

**Cara a Cara.** The investigation game — ask questions to eliminate faces until only theirs remains.

## 🧠 Meaning

The game is information gathering. Every question should cut the remaining faces roughly in half.

## 🗝️ Beat it with ease

- **Split the board, not the people.** Ask about the most common feature first — hair color, glasses, facial hair — so the largest group flips down.
- **Always binary search.** After each answer, the field should shrink by ~50%; if a question only removes two faces, it was wasted.
- **Ask about big categories early.** "Does your person have glasses?" is often better than "Is your person Sam?" — never guess a name until the field is tiny.
- **Ride the biggest flip.** If a question answers your way, follow with the next most common feature in that surviving group.
- **Keep a mental shortlist.** Track the faces still standing; guess only when you have one or two left.
