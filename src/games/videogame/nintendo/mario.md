# 🍄 Mario

## 🧠 Meaning

The jump-and-run toybox — movement mastery as the whole product, story as an excuse for a new verb. Played heavily up to the Wii; the mainline loop started feeling routine, and enjoyment faded at the
Wii U era.

> 🚪 **Retired from Nintendo from the Wii U era onward** — titles marked 🔒 **launched after** the Wii and were not played.
