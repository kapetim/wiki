# 🎮 Nintendo

## 🧠 Meaning

Breadth-first, fun-first franchise lane — the Nintendo board is a **played catalog**, not a genre rulebook. The era was heavy on N64, GameCube, and Wii; a wide catalog played, only some finished.

> 🚪 **Retired from Nintendo from the Wii U era onward** — everything on this page is up to the Wii. Titles marked 🔒 **launched after** the Wii and were not played.

Franchise pages: [mario.md](./mario.md) · [pokemon.md](./pokemon.md)
