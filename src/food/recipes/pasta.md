# 🍗 Meat + pasta

Sear meat well, toss with pasta. The core weekday meal — one meat, rotated.

## 🧹 Equipment

- Stove
- Pan (for meat)
- Pot (for pasta)
- Colander
- Knife
- Cooking spoon
- Cutting board

## 🥥 Ingredients

- Chicken breast / beef / pork loin (rotate)
- Dry pasta
- Soy oil
- Water (for pasta)
- Salt (for pasta water)
- Seasoning: general blend to taste (see seasoning.md)

Quantities per item: see [ingredient registry](../diet/ingredients.md).

## 🧹 Method

1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Cook pasta al dente. Drain.
2. While pasta cooks, season the day's meat. Sear in a splash of **soy oil** in a pan over heat until golden and cooked through.
3. Slice or shred the meat. Toss with pasta.
4. Adjust seasoning with general blend.

> Standard soy oil under heat — no olive oil.
