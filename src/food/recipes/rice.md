# 🍚 Rice + liver + sardines + eggs

One-pot — rice cooks in the same pan, absorbing meat and egg flavor. All-in-one nutritious weekend meal.

## 🧹 Equipment

- Stove
- Pan
- Knife
- Fork
- Spoon
- Plate
- Can opener

## 🥥 Ingredients

- Canned sardines in oil
- Bovine liver
- Eggs
- Dry white rice
- Water
- Seasoning: general blend

Quantities per item: see [ingredient registry](../diet/ingredients.md).

## 🧹 Method

1. Pour **sardines** into a pan. Press with a spoon or fork to shred.
2. While sardines heat up, chop **liver** on a plate into bite-sized pieces.
3. Add **liver** to the pan. Sear together ~3 min.
4. Crack **eggs** over the mix. Scramble.
5. Add **water** and **rice**. Season with general blend. Use about **one part rice to two parts water** — pour a cup of rice, add two cups of water.
6. Stir, cover, and simmer until rice is tender (~15 min, add water as needed).
