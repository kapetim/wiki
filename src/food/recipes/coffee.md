# 🫖 Black coffee

Brew and drink. No sugar, no milk.

## 🧹 Equipment

- Stove
- Pan
- Paper filter plastic holder
- Mug

## 🥥 Ingredients

- Coffee
- Water
- Paper filter

Quantities per item: see [ingredient registry](../diet/ingredients.md).

## 🧹 Method

1. Heat **water** in a pan on the stove until just off boiling.
2. Place **paper filter** in the plastic holder over a mug.
3. Add a **reasonable amount of ground coffee** for a strong mug.
4. Slowly pour **hot water** over the grounds. Let it drip through.
