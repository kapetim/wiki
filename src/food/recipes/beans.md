# 🥣 Black beans

Pressure cook, optionally blend. Weekday meal — 5×/wk.

## 🧹 Equipment

- Pressure cooker
- Cooking spoon
- Bowl (for optional soaking)

## 🥥 Ingredients

- Dry black beans
- Water
- Seasoning: general blend (see seasoning.md)

Quantities per item: see [ingredient registry](../diet/ingredients.md).

## 🧹 Method

1. Rinse beans. Optional soak (30 min) speeds cooking.
2. Add beans to pressure cooker with enough water to cover by ~3cm.
3. Pressure cook 25-40 minutes until very soft.
4. Season with general blend. Mash some against the pot wall for thickness, or blend smooth.
