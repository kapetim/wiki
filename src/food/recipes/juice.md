# 🥤 Detox juice

Half lemon, half beetroot, sunflower seeds, water. Good for dinner — curbs evening overeating. 5×/wk.

## 🧹 Equipment

- Blender
- Knife
- Cutting board
- Measuring cup
- Glass

## 🥥 Ingredients

- Beetroot
- Lemon
- Sunflower seeds
- Water

Quantities per item: see [ingredient registry](../diet/ingredients.md).

## 🧹 Method

1. Wash everything. Chop beetroot into chunks.
2. Add all ingredients to a blender with enough water to blend smooth.
3. Blend until smooth. Drink fresh.
