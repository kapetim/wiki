# 🪙 Karma — the accounting system

**What this is:** karma as **accounting** — every person carries a **life log** with two columns: what they **deserve
and have not received**, and what they **should have paid and have not**. When the ledger cannot pay, **vengeance
masquerades as a payment** ([fair-game.md](fair-game.md) runs the trial).

## 🧾 The ledger — two sides

Every person carries a running account:

<!-- begin table -->
| Column | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Receivable** | what they **deserve but have not received** |
| **Payable** | what they **should have paid but have not** |
<!-- end table -->

- **Easy cases** — a **restaurant** has well-defined prices and payment methods, and the **police** run after people
  who do not pay the bill.
- **Hard truth** — most crimes carry **no consequence**: no receipt, no counterparty, no enforcer.

## ⚖️ Vengeance masked as payment

When the ledger has no enforcer, **vengeance dresses up as payment** — the victim tries to collect by hand. It rarely
balances the books; it usually adds a line ([society.md § fix, justice, vengeance](../observation/society/society.md)).

## 🕯️ Closed cases — judged impossible

Events that **already happened and cannot be judged**, because the people who could owe or collect are **already dead**:

<!-- begin table -->
| Event | Date | Scale | Score | Why unjudgeable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bin Laden / 9-11** | 2001-09-11 (killed 2011-05-02) | 2,977 victims (2,996 incl. 19 hijackers) | 1 : 2,977 | both sides dead — no equal response exists |
| **TAM 3054 / Conçongas** | 2007-07-17 | 199 dead (187 aboard + 12 ground) | — | rainy-runway overrun — no culprit left to charge |
<!-- end table -->

These are a **record of what already happened**, not a verdict ([hard-limit.md](../observation/fundamentals/hard-limit.md)).

## 🌫️ The grey area

Between the easy cases and the closed ones sits the **grey area**: the culprit is **unknown and possibly alive**, but
there is **no proof** — at best a **camera** showing a masked person.

- The ledger says **owed**; the evidence says **maybe**
  ([online.md](../problems/unknown/online.md), [surveillance.md](../problems/unknown/surveillance.md)).

## ❓ Open questions

- Who collects a debt whose owner is dead?
- Is a camera enough to move a life from *never* to *maybe*?
